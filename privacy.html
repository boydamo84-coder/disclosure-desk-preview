Disclosure Desk Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy describes how Disclosure Desk handles information when you use the website or Android application. Contact: support@uapdisclosuredesk.com.

Information stored on your device

Disclosure Desk uses browser or app local storage for settings and activity such as saved investigation board copies, research links and notes, news interests, favourites, reading history, some draft witness reports and the identifier of a recently opened W.NE Research Edition document. Some items remain only on your device. Clearing browser or app data may remove local information. Signed-in cloud documents and backups are described separately below. When you export or share a file, review its contents before sending it.

Optional accounts, private Boards and W.NE Research Edition

You may explore the public website or the W.NE guest demonstration without creating an account. Guest preview content is fictional sample material and does not load anyone's private research.

If you register or sign in, Supabase handles account authentication. Your account email and authentication information are handled by that service. Signed-in features may store private investigation Boards, research documents, titles, sections, references and backup copies in the Disclosure Desk Supabase project. Access controls are designed to restrict records to their owners, but no internet service can promise absolute security. Some older copies, ideas and exports remain stored locally rather than automatically synchronised.

Research Edition may remember a last-opened document identifier for your signed-in account in local browser storage, so the workspace can reopen a document you are authorised to access. An identifier does not grant access to another person's research. You can clear the local record by clearing site data.

Research Edition is in beta. Keep an independent copy of important research and verify saved changes, exports and recovery before deleting an original. Avoid submitting confidential information to untested features.

Hypothesis Lab (R3) encrypted notebooks and cloud backup

The Hypothesis Lab is a beta research worksheet. New work remains in browser memory until you enable passphrase-encrypted browser saving; each completed revision is encrypted locally. When signed in to your Disclosure Desk account, you may explicitly save a separate encrypted notebook backup to your account in Supabase. Only its encrypted data, a random version marker, an account identifier and technical timestamps are stored in the cloud. We do not receive your notebook passphrase and cannot reset it or read your encrypted research through the normal backup service.

Cloud backup is optional and manual, not continuous synchronisation. An account may retain one current encrypted backup, which can be replaced or deleted by that account holder through the Lab. Deleting the cloud backup does not delete a local notebook or a previously downloaded encrypted file. Deleting the account may also remove its cloud backup. Browser storage is scoped to the signed-in profile for this feature, but anyone with access to your unlocked device or your passphrase may pose a risk. Avoid confidential details, precise witness locations and other sensitive information while the beta undergoes independent security review.

Optional Google Drive Word copies

Where the Google Drive connection is enabled, you can choose to connect your own Google account and manually upload a readable Word copy of your open research document. Google receives the document and its title only when you request a backup. These Word files are not password-encrypted archives. The connection requests access to files created or authorised through this app rather than your whole Drive. It uses a temporary access token held in memory, not saved in Disclosure Desk's database or browser storage. We store the time of the last verified backup in this browser. Disconnecting stops the current connection; it does not delete files already saved in your Drive. You can manage those files and revoke access in your Google account. Drive availability depends on the website connection being configured; local Word downloads remain available independently.

Watch & Read link suggestions

When you add a link, its category, title and URL appear publicly after validation and duplicate checks. We also store your account identifier, submission time and any optional note; account identifiers and notes are not included in the public catalogue. Reports store the reporting account, reason and time, and reporter identities are not public. Five reports from different registered members hide the item for owner review; the owner may restore or remove it. Review actions are retained for moderation. Do not submit private research or personal information as a public title or link. Contact support if you need an addition removed.

Congressional LIVE hearings and signed-in chat

We display verified public hearing schedules, countdowns and official stream or recording links. These dates and streams originate from published Congressional or committee sources and may change. Video playback through official or YouTube embeds can transmit connection details to the video provider when you choose to play a video.

If you choose to participate in a LIVE hearing chat using a registered Disclosure Desk account, we store your messages, a pseudonymous author identifier, the hearing room and the posting time. Other signed-in participants can see these messages. We do not display your email address in chat. Posts may be reported to moderators and hidden for safety, spam, abuse or privacy reasons. Automated rate limits apply. Please do not share identifying witness details, private research or personal contact information in public discussions.

LIVE chat is restricted to an officially approved hearing window. Historical hearings may continue to offer a separate discussion archive. Contact support to request assistance with your chat data, subject to applicable legal obligations.

Spelling and grammar assistance

If you use W.NE's writing checks, the text being checked is sent from your signed-in browser to an authenticated Supabase function, which forwards it to our LanguageTool service hosted on Oracle Cloud infrastructure. This is needed to return spelling, grammar, punctuation and writing-style suggestions. Only submit text you are comfortable having processed by these services; avoid passwords, confidential reports and identifying witness details. Provider infrastructure may process connection information under its applicable terms and policies.

Suggested corrections are offered for your review and are not automatically applied to the original document.

Website hosting and technical data

Our website is hosted by Netlify. Hosting services may process technical information such as IP addresses, request times, browser details and security logs to serve the website, protect it and troubleshoot problems. Refer to Netlify's privacy information.

News, video and external services

Disclosure Desk links to third-party news publishers, archives and video providers, including YouTube. Opening their websites or playing embedded media may allow those providers to process information under their own privacy policies. Map features and other external resources may make requests to their providers. Third-party content is not controlled by Disclosure Desk.

Website traffic and search measurement

Disclosure Desk uses Netlify Web Analytics to understand visits and requested pages using hosting traffic data. Netlify Web Analytics does not need an additional Google Analytics browser tracking script or analytics cookies. Hosting services may process technical information such as IP address, request time and browser information for this purpose. Internal navigation between tabs on a single page is not necessarily measured as separate pageviews. See Netlify’s privacy information for details. Google Search Console may separately provide aggregate information about how this website appears in Google Search.

Google Analytics 4 is being retired from Disclosure Desk with this website release. This version no longer loads GA4 or offers a separate Google Analytics consent choice. Previously collected data in the Google account is not erased by removing the tag, and older cookies on returning devices can remain until expiry or browser cleanup. Advertising consent remains separate and is described below.

Advertising and consent

Disclosure Desk is preparing optional, clearly labelled standard display advertising on public News, Archive, Videos and Discussions sections using Adsterra. Adsterra may use cookies or similar storage/access technologies, process IP addresses and device information and collect advert interactions for delivery, measurement or fraud prevention. Adsterra advertising is not yet enabled on this website; no Adsterra script is loaded by this staging configuration. If enabled after review, advertising consent will be requested separately from analytics consent, with equally available Allow adverts and No thanks choices. Refusing will not limit access to public content or W.NE. Ads will not appear in W.NE, private documents, Chat or research tools. You may revisit your advertising choice below and withdraw it to stop future advert requests; previously set third-party cookies may require clearing browser data. Ad quality, provider disclosures and UK consent compliance must be checked before any public activation. Google AdSense may be considered separately, and the Android app may use AdMob in a future release.

Public usernames and private Chat

During account registration you choose a username. After you sign in, this username is public and searchable by other members for Chat invitations by default, while your email address remains private. Invitations are not accepted automatically; you decide whether to accept, decline, block or report. You can hide the username or disable incoming Chat requests in Profile at any time. Previously saved visibility and contact choices are not reset by signing in.

Member profile photographs

Registered members may upload or change a profile image, which saves automatically when selected. We resize and re-encode it as a small WebP and do not store the original upload. By default, your uploaded photo may appear beside your chosen public username in Chat and Discussions. You can switch off “Show my profile picture to others” in Profile at any time, or remove the photo entirely. Private Storage permissions then prevent new public downloads, but copies already downloaded or saved by other visitors cannot be recalled. Your sign-in email and account identifier are not included in the public photo lookup.

Messages and support

If you email us about general enquiries, source corrections, W.NE beta feedback or technical support, we receive the email address and message you send. We use this information to respond and handle the enquiry. Our public support address forwards to a managed mailbox. Please do not email passwords, private research, sensitive personal information or precise witness addresses.

Witness reports and location privacy

Disclosure Desk aims to show reported incidents at town or city level, rather than publishing exact addresses or precise witness locations. Draft reports may remain locally on your device. Do not include identifying details about other people in reports or notes you choose to share.

Request deletion of your Disclosure Desk account

Email our support team from your account email address to request deletion of your account and associated data. You can also start the request from Profile → Account & Security → Delete my account in the Android app. Export any research you want to keep before requesting deletion.

Request account deletion by email. Support may ask you to confirm account ownership. Please never send your password or research passphrase.

Your choices and rights

You can remove locally stored notes, favourites, history and last-opened research document identifiers using available controls or by clearing site or app data. Clearing local data does not necessarily delete signed-in cloud documents or your account. You may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information we hold, subject to applicable law. To ask a privacy question or exercise a right, email support@uapdisclosuredesk.com. If applicable, you may also complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Disclosure Desk features, service providers and advertising arrangements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
