Disclosure Desk Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026

This policy describes how Disclosure Desk handles information when you use the website or Android application. Contact: support@uapdisclosuredesk.com.

Information stored on your device

Disclosure Desk uses browser or app local storage for settings and activity such as saved investigation board copies, research links and notes, news interests, favourites, reading history, some draft witness reports and the identifier of a recently opened W.NE Research Edition document. Some items remain only on your device. Clearing browser or app data may remove local information. Signed-in cloud documents and backups are described separately below. When you export or share a file, review its contents before sending it.

Optional accounts, private Boards and W.NE Research Edition

You may explore the public website or the W.NE guest demonstration without creating an account. Guest preview content is fictional sample material and does not load anyone's private research.

If you register or sign in, Supabase handles account authentication. Your account email and authentication information are handled by that service. Signed-in features may store private investigation Boards, research documents, titles, sections, references and backup copies in the Disclosure Desk Supabase project. Access controls are designed to restrict records to their owners, but no internet service can promise absolute security. Some older copies, ideas and exports remain stored locally rather than automatically synchronised.

Research Edition may remember a last-opened document identifier for your signed-in account in local browser storage, so the workspace can reopen a document you are authorised to access. An identifier does not grant access to another person's research. You can clear the local record by clearing site data.

Research Edition is in beta. Keep an independent copy of important research and verify saved changes, exports and recovery before deleting an original. Avoid submitting confidential information to untested features.

Spelling and grammar assistance

If you use W.NE's writing checks, the text being checked is sent from your signed-in browser to an authenticated Supabase function, which forwards it to our LanguageTool service hosted on Oracle Cloud infrastructure. This is needed to return spelling, grammar, punctuation and writing-style suggestions. Only submit text you are comfortable having processed by these services; avoid passwords, confidential reports and identifying witness details. Provider infrastructure may process connection information under its applicable terms and policies.

Suggested corrections are offered for your review and are not automatically applied to the original document.

Website hosting and technical data

Our website is hosted by Netlify. Hosting services may process technical information such as IP addresses, request times, browser details and security logs to serve the website, protect it and troubleshoot problems. Refer to Netlify's privacy information.

News, video and external services

Disclosure Desk links to third-party news publishers, archives and video providers, including YouTube. Opening their websites or playing embedded media may allow those providers to process information under their own privacy policies. Map features and other external resources may make requests to their providers. Third-party content is not controlled by Disclosure Desk.

Advertising and consent

The website may display advertising through Google AdSense, while the Android app is being prepared to use Google AdMob. Where advertising is enabled, providers may process device identifiers, cookies where permitted, IP addresses and ad-interaction details to serve and measure ads and help prevent fraud. Consent and preference controls depend on the service, region and applicable requirements. Where consent is required, it must be requested before non-essential advertising or tracking. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy. Advertising may not be active in every app or page.

Messages and support

If you email us about general enquiries, source corrections, W.NE beta feedback or technical support, we receive the email address and message you send. We use this information to respond and handle the enquiry. Our public support address forwards to a managed mailbox. Please do not email passwords, private research, sensitive personal information or precise witness addresses.

Witness reports and location privacy

Disclosure Desk aims to show reported incidents at town or city level, rather than publishing exact addresses or precise witness locations. Draft reports may remain locally on your device. Do not include identifying details about other people in reports or notes you choose to share.

Your choices and rights

You can remove locally stored notes, favourites, history and last-opened research document identifiers using available controls or by clearing site or app data. Clearing local data does not necessarily delete signed-in cloud documents or your account. You may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information we hold, subject to applicable law. To ask a privacy question or exercise a right, email support@uapdisclosuredesk.com. If applicable, you may also complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Disclosure Desk features, service providers and advertising arrangements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
