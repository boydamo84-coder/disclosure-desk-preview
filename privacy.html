Disclosure Desk Privacy Policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026

This policy describes how Disclosure Desk handles information when you use the website or Android application. Contact: support@uapdisclosuredesk.com.

Information stored on your device

Disclosure Desk uses browser or app local storage to remember settings and activity such as saved investigation boards, research links, notes, news interests, favourites, read history and some draft witness reports. This information is generally stored on your device rather than uploaded to a Disclosure Desk user account. Clearing app or browser data may remove it. If you choose to export or share information, you control the resulting file or share action.

Optional account and cloud investigation boards

If you create an optional Disclosure Desk account, our authentication provider Supabase processes your email address, account credentials and authentication information to provide sign-in and account recovery. If you choose to copy investigation boards to the cloud, your board names, saved research links and notes are stored in our Supabase project and associated with your account. Cloud upload and download are manual choices; existing local boards are not automatically replaced. Avoid storing sensitive information about other people in your boards. Contact support@uapdisclosuredesk.com about account or cloud data requests.

Website hosting and technical data

Our website is hosted by Netlify. Hosting services may process technical information such as IP addresses, request times, browser details and security logs to serve the website, protect it and troubleshoot problems. Refer to Netlify's privacy information.

News, video and external services

Disclosure Desk links to third-party news publishers, archives and video providers, including YouTube. Opening their websites or playing embedded media may allow those providers to process information under their own privacy policies. Map features and other external resources may make requests to their providers. Third-party content is not controlled by Disclosure Desk.

Advertising and consent

The Android app is being prepared to use Google AdMob to display advertising. Where enabled, advertising providers may process device identifiers, IP addresses and information about ad interactions for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention, subject to applicable requirements and your choices. For users in regions requiring consent, the app will request consent through Google's User Messaging Platform where appropriate and offer a way to revisit choices. We will not treat silence as consent. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy. Google AdSense website advertising is currently being prepared and is subject to site approval. If enabled, it may use cookies or similar technologies for ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention according to applicable consent choices. Website visitors in applicable regions will be offered advertising privacy choices through an appropriate consent management platform. Website adverts are not currently confirmed as active. Advertising is not yet necessarily active in every version of the app.

Messages and support

If you contact support by email, we receive the details you send, including your email address and message. We use this information to respond to your request and handle related support matters. Our public support address forwards to a managed mailbox. Please do not email sensitive personal information or precise witness addresses.

Witness reports and location privacy

Disclosure Desk aims to show reported incidents at town or city level, rather than publishing exact addresses or precise witness locations. Draft reports may remain locally on your device. Do not include identifying details about other people in reports or notes you choose to share.

Your choices and rights

You can remove locally stored notes, favourites and history using available app controls or by clearing the app's data. You may have rights to request access, correction, deletion or restriction of personal information we hold, subject to applicable law. To ask a privacy question or exercise a right, email support@uapdisclosuredesk.com. If applicable, you may also complain to the UK's Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as Disclosure Desk features, service providers and advertising arrangements change. The latest version will be published on this page with its updated date.
