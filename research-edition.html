What is Research Studio?

Rather than keeping evidence notes, source links and report drafts scattered across different apps, the Research Edition brings your investigation into a focused writing environment.

  • Section-based reports help you separate the overview, evidence, analysis and unresolved questions.
  • A side-by-side workspace makes it easier to consult supporting references while working on your report.
  • Investigation Boards and citations connect research notes with source material and comparisons.
  • Writing checks offer British English spelling, grammar and punctuation suggestions through an authenticated LanguageTool service.
  • Export options help create copies of your work for review and safe keeping.

Beta and privacy information

Research Edition is being tested on mobile and desktop. Before trusting important research to the beta, verify saved documents, exports and recovery with disposable material. Keep an independent backup of irreplaceable work.

If you use live writing assistance, the text being checked is sent to the authenticated LanguageTool service hosted on Oracle infrastructure. Avoid sending confidential or identifying witness details. Read our Privacy Policy to understand the data processing involved.
