## Ross Coulthart — July 2023 reported craft/body recoveries

**Original claimed statement:** The profile links the 7NEWS Australia 26 July 2023 broadcast [video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x_9gTDXF9Vc), in which Coulthart says the U.S. is concealing recovered non-human craft, technology and alleged bodies. Direct YouTube playback/text was inaccessible in this research pass. The separately produced [ABC Australia Media Watch transcript, 28 August 2023](https://www.abc.net.au/mediawatch/episodes/ufos/102785078), reproduces his July 7NEWS statement and identifies a **different**, earlier NewsNation interview with David Grusch from June 2023. **Limited direct-original verification:** confirm the full 7NEWS recording before second editorial signoff; the cross-quotation currently verifies the *attribution* of the assertion.

**Independence:** Coulthart's Grusch reporting, the [26 July 2023 official House transcript](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-118hhrg53022/html/CHRG-118hhrg53022.htm), and ABC's recap are overlapping reporting/testimony chains, not three independently examined physical specimens. **Counter/context:** ABC challenged the public evidentiary presentation; the [DoD/AARO March 2024 historical summary](https://www.defense.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/Article/3701297/dod-report-discounts-sightings-of-extraterrestrial-technology/) reported no verified extraterrestrial recovery technology in the cases it examined. This cannot conclusively dispose of every withheld allegation.

**Needed:** the directly reviewed full original video, distinguishable named firsthand evidence, physical sample or official programme-authentication records, independently reproducible test/custody data and a separately assigned reviewer. **Status:** not assessed; no 0–100 finding.

## Jeremy Corbell — 2021 USS Omaha Navy-video interpretation

**Original first-person source:** [CNN broadcast transcript for 31 May 2021](https://transcripts.cnn.com/show/ebo/date/2021-05-31/segment/01), approximately after its **19:50:35** marker, hosted by Poppy Harlow. Corbell told CNN of a purported 2019 ship encounter with **more than 14** UFOs, describing the footage as demonstrating technology beyond known next-generation capabilities and referring to an apparently water-entering object.

**What independent confirmation actually supports:** CNN quoted DoD spokeswoman Susan Gough confirming photos/videos were **recorded by Navy personnel** and that the incidents were under UAP Task Force examination. The response did **not** confirm Corbell's performance estimates, a verified water-to-air transition, extraordinary propulsion or object origin. CNN labels the material a **rush transcript**, so an exact broadcast quote should be checked against source audio. The [DoD's separate 27 April 2020 historical Navy video release](https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/release/article/2165713/statement-by-the-department-of-defense-on-the-release-of-historical-navy-videos/) relates to **other, earlier Navy clips** and must **not** be portrayed as official validation of the 2019 Omaha imagery.

**Competing possibilities:** CNN relayed Scott Kelly's optical or unmanned-aircraft suggestion; science reporting raised limits of apparent video disappearance and perspective. Neither is a decisive USS Omaha sensor reconstruction. Corbell/Knapp releases of related footage are not independent experiment repeats.

**Needed:** original 2019 radar tracks, raw visual masters, time alignment, sensor calibration, target ranges, operations logs, independent demonstration of crossing the water and second-editor audit. **Status:** not assessed; no 0–100 finding.

## Steven Greer — 9 May 2001 Disclosure Project press conference

**Original contemporaneous records:** [ABC News original event coverage](https://abcnews.go.com/Technology/story?id=98572&page=1) records Greer's National Press Club press-conference assertion that testimony establishes extraterrestrial presence and that approximately **400 people were potential witnesses**. [The Washington Post's on-scene report of 10 May 2001](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/lifestyle/2001/05/10/ufo-believers-sighted-here/f5b0fb0e-80f6-4d7e-8c25-e57d1aa69135/) separately corroborates event occurrence, Greer's overarching public argument and about twenty speakers present. [WorldCat catalogue entry for an edited 2001 event VHS](https://search.worldcat.org/title/52741633) establishes preservation/publication of a video record, **not** proof of the witness claims; the full recording was not examined in this pass.

**What that establishes:** An organized press conference and attributed claims, not an independently audited list of 400 signed eyewitness depositions or an alien artefact. Some speakers described the same eras or related narratives and should not be counted as automatically independent corroboration.

**Competing account:** The Washington Post's contemporary reporter found no forensic exhibit presented at the conference. ABC quoted James Oberg questioning Greer's expansive concealment theory while urging proper documentation of genuine unexplained observations. These concerns are not blanket personal judgements or proof that each speaker's experience was false.

**Needed:** full original event recording, names and source-specific contemporaneous duty records, independent accounts and sample/sensor evidence for each alleged incident, authenticated primary exhibits and second-reviewer attribution checks. **Status:** not assessed; no 0–100 finding.

## George Knapp — September 2025 House written retrieval-programme assertion

**Original signed/source record:** [Knapp's 9 September 2025 written testimony to the House task force](https://oversight.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/George-Knapp-Written-Testimony.pdf). The original source was checked against rendered PDF pages **1–3** (cover text, "Topic One: The Paper Trail", "Topic Two: Crash Retrievals and Reverse Engineering Programs (They're Real)"). He explicitly says he is an **investigative reporter, not a UFO witness or whistleblower**. The [House hearing page](https://oversight.house.gov/hearing/restoring-public-trust-through-uap-transparency-and-whistleblower-protection/) independently confirms his scheduled witness role.

**What he asserts:** Retells his 1989 Bob Lazar/S-4 reporting, more than two dozen contacts, claimed intimidation of six newsroom contacts, and later Reid/Cannon/Goldwater/Al O'Donnell accounts. These are source interviews and accounts of conversations; the public PDF does not attach a recovered sample or original authorized reverse-engineering plan. The 2025 retelling is **not independent of the underlying 1989 Lazar reporting**.

**Counter/context:** [Nevada Public Radio/KNPR's 2018 Knapp–Robert Stoldal discussion](https://www.knpr.org/show/knprs-state-of-nevada/2018-12-13/a-new-film-focuses-on-bob-lazars-area-51-story-but-is-it-for-real) examines unresolved education/work checks around Lazar; these are not automatic disproof of other sources. [AARO's 2024 historical review](https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/AARO_Historical_Record_Report_Volume_1_2024.pdf) supplies an institutional contrary assessment of examined hidden-programme claims, but predates the 2025 testimony and is not an independently witnessed Knapp-specific dismissal.

**Needed:** original broadcast and notes, source identities/independence, verified Reid/Cannon/Goldwater communications, documentary evidence for programme and alleged intimidation, physical evidence/custody and second editorial signoff. **Status:** not assessed; no 0–100 finding.
