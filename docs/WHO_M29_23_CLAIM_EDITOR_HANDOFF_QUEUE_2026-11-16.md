## What would make a claim eligible for an approval decision?

See [M29 editorial review protocol](WHO_M29_EDITORIAL_REVIEW_PROTOCOL_2026-11-16.md). A genuine independent human reviewer must inspect available original and competing evidence, identify what could not be accessed, examine any applicable physical/technical source provenance and complete the blank human-review form with conflict-of-interest declaration and real signed decision. The owner must then separately grant publication approval. **No editor may be assumed to exist merely because these pages are generated.**

For an incident with inadequate evidence, the responsible disposition may remain `Not assessed`; a 0/100 score would misleadingly imply strong evidence of falsity. For policy/records-access recommendations (Christopher Mellon and Mike Gold), incident scoring is explicitly **Not applicable**.

The proposed community member percentage, if ever approved, reflects separate signed-in user assessments. It is not an automated corroboration, editorial decision, truthfulness metric or substitute for official data. Secure two-account live authentication, RLS/uniqueness/rate/cooldown QA and privacy review are still required.
