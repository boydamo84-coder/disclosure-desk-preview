## Owner decision: auto-publication and five flags (supersedes pre-moderation)

Valid consented text-only submissions and revisions auto-publish. Unscanned attachments remain disabled. Each published version can receive one flag per signed-in non-anonymous non-author member per review round, with a reason. Five distinct flags automatically hide that version from public reads and engagement; a moderator may restore it (fresh flag round, old records retained privately) or reject it. Moderator review cannot be performed by the author. Flag and moderation records remain private; the moderator queue exposes reasons and counts only. A provisional ten-flags-per-24-hours limit reduces spam. The entire work can still be withdrawn by its author. Other versions remain citable unless independently hidden or withdrawn. The local UI simulates these transitions, not actual accounts or publication. Test run 45757744136 passed the prior pre-moderation design; newer combined tests are required. Profile identity, genuine account and anti-brigading acceptance, attachment scanning, UI integration and deployment gates remain outstanding. No production changes; researchPublishing:false remains in force.
