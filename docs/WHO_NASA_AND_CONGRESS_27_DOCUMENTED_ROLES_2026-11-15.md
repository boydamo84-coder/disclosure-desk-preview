**Critical distinction:** Proposed special review-board / eminent-domain mechanisms in earlier versions must not be advertised as enacted in full. A law that mandates records collection neither determines the authenticity of individual source material nor automatically declassifies all government-held information.

## How a visitor will inspect the records

The existing premium Who's Who profile displays a new expandable **Inspect original role & legislative records** panel when a role has a properly formed event/date, category, explanation and at least **two** safe original HTTPS citations. For each item it shows: exact publisher/document name, where to look, source check date, explanation of the evidence's *limited* relevance, and a direct original-document link.

Profiles with separate allegations still retain their distinct **View original evidence sources (review pending)** or future **View evidence & scoring breakdown** areas. This prevents roster/legislative facts from being mixed into calculations of supporting evidence for an extraordinary physical claim. The role reader is available to guests: **no login or vote** needed.

If a citation becomes unsafe or missing, the role card fails closed (no misleading two-source claim). Future work should maintain source mirrors/archival information with permission, independent per-statement review and explicit editorial versioning. Any rights-sensitive imagery stays with the original site until cleared.
