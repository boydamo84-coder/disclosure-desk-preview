## Purpose and release boundary

The main live/public Who's Who data retains **50 original profiles**. M58 introduced six *staged* additional biographies in `data/public-people-directory-additions-m58.json`. M59 selectively loads **only `sean-kirkpatrick`** from that secondary JSON into the development reader (51 visible if the optional source file is present and valid). The addition is deliberately source-first. Kirkpatrick headed AARO from its 2022 establishment until his December 2023 retirement; subsequent commentary is **not the position of current AARO director Jon Kosloski**. Do not label anyone a liar or imply a documented clash proves knowing deception.

## Three distinct kinds of document

- **Official record:** Department of Defense establishment and departure announcements; April and October 2023 AARO director statements; AARO FOIA index and published correspondence.
- **Other named people's public accounts:** Grusch's sworn July 2023 testimony and subsequent attributed claims. These establish what the witness said, not independently the classified programme's existence.
- **Former-director commentary:** Kirkpatrick's 2024 Scientific American essay, 2024 podcast and the 2026 UAP Files and Fox News appearances. Do not mistake these for current AARO official releases or direct source material for every case.

## Read these points in parallel, not as personal verdicts

### A. Initial contact versus a completed AARO interview

**Statement 1:** In the official July 26, 2023 House transcript, David Grusch told Rep. Luna he spoke to Kirkpatrick in **April 2022**, *before* AARO was established, and said he had raised concerns. Original at [GovInfo House hearing](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-118hhrg53022/html/CHRG-118hhrg53022.htm), lines 1433–1446 in the indexed HTML.

**Statement 2:** At an [official Department of Defense media roundtable, October 31, 2023](https://www.defense.gov/News/Transcripts/Transcript/Article/3575588/aaro-director-dr-sean-kirkpatrick-holds-an-off-camera-media-roundtable/), Kirkpatrick addressed whether Grusch had come to AARO and supplied information *since the office's creation*.

**Further documentary trail:** [AARO 24-F-0266 FOIA indexed PDF](https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/FOIA/2024%20FOIAs/24-F-0266.pdf) includes a later memorandum and records of attempts to arrange a formal interview. Index confirms the release; **full PDF page-image inspection remains outstanding** in this review.

**Interpretation restriction:** Earlier personal contact, intermediary messages, an invitation, a scheduled meeting, completed secure disclosure, and evidence actually transferred are **six distinct facts**. Nothing in these sources by itself demonstrates Kirkpatrick's intention to deceive. Do not count a disagreement as a resolved contradiction unless the *exact same assertion, time frame and meaning* are matched.

### B. Scope of the official 'no verified off-world technology' statement

In [DoD's 19 April 2023 Senate testimony summary](https://www.defense.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/article/3368109/dod-working-to-better-understand-resolve-anomalous-phenomena/), Kirkpatrick described AARO's then-evaluated reports. In the [July 26, 2023 House transcript](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-118hhrg53022/html/CHRG-118hhrg53022.htm), at approximately lines 1634–1649, Grusch directly contested Kirkpatrick's description of the evidence available to AARO and said some people with whom he spoke had approached the office.

**Research question:** What did AARO have, what did it actually review, what did the witnesses provide, and what remained uncorroborated or classified at those precise dates? The distinction is *not* the simplistic claim that AARO disproved all UAP incidents. A disagreement between public statements does not independently establish which underlying classified assertion was correct.

### C. AARO's 2024 historical assessment versus his subsequent personal analysis

[Kirkpatrick's original January 2024 Scientific American essay](https://www.scientificamerican.com/article/heres-what-i-learned-as-the-u-s-governments-ufo-hunter/) is clearly a **former director's first-person opinion and interpretation** after leaving office. AARO's official March 2024 historical report and later statements belong to the **institution**, after his departure. Keep original report authorship and agency authority separate from his later commentary; source lineage must be traced rather than merging every report and retelling into one independent proof.

### D. Continued 2026 media engagement is not current agency authority

- [29 May 2026 UAP Files original publisher video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=q-IncaY7zQ8) — an extended interview of a **former** AARO director, concerning Nimitz, sensors and other claims. Publisher identification is confirmed; do not assume any new raw-data audit happened simply because topics were discussed.
- [29 October 2026 UAP Files podcast segment](https://podcasts.apple.com/us/podcast/ex-pentagon-ufo-chief-nothing-credible-about-skywatcher/id1741618111?i=1000798770539) — Kirkpatrick's quoted view on Skywatcher, not independently verified truth of the entire case.
- [31 October 2026 Fox News original interview video](https://www.foxnews.com/video/6407612320112) and [1 November 2026 Fox News report](https://www.foxnews.com/us/former-pentagon-ufo-chief-disputes-fbi-black-triangle-sighting-says-reports-lack-evidence): interviewer identifies him as **former** director, asking about newly released reports. He argued missing supporting evidence prevents strong conclusions. Read the original released government records separately before evaluating the factual basis.
- [AARO's current director page](https://www.aaro.mil/About/Leaders/) lists **Dr. Jon T. Kosloski**. Neither a former-director title nor his continuing media visibility confers current agency authority.

## Source-quality status and boundaries

This review used primary government **HTML indexed text** for the 2023 House hearing, official agency search/index results for some DoD/AARO pages (direct pages can return HTTP 403), public first-party publisher pages for his own statements, and the original 2026 media listings. **No full FOIA PDF or source-video frame-by-frame audit is claimed.** Link rot, missing context, unverified audiovisual timestamps, legal/portrait permissions and scope of classified access remain open research issues.

The public site should show the full source list and labelled scope, not an editorial personal score, extraordinary-origin verdict, or eligibility for member voting. Case association (e.g., Grusch exchange) is a dispute over agency contacts, **not a new verified UAP encounter or case-to-case relation**.
