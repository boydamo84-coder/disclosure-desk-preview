## Why M28d differs from the earlier hearing and journal checks
The original television or publisher master was unavailable or not playable for all four original broadcaster claims. This pass deliberately does **not** label any of those as a watched-and-transcribed full original. Instead it records exact alternative sources and what each can actually establish: an independent broadcaster *quoting* an assertion, an original newsroom's syndicated excerpt, a third-party historical retyped broadcast, and official historical archive guidance.

Access labels must remain visible in the evidence reader, and reading multiple republications of the **same story** must never be counted as independent witness corroboration. A media outlet's editorial stance, archived rating, source's prominence, or a number of clicks is not a measure of a person's honesty or the underlying incident's truth.

- **Original official archival reference checked visually:** The UK National Archives, *Briefing document on records regarding unidentified flying objects*, revised **June 2012**, https://cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk/documents/briefing-guide-12-07-12.pdf . **Printed page 13, zero-indexed PDF page 12 visually inspected on 16 November 2026**. These are dated original *archive guidance*; they **do not independently confirm Warren's attendance on the precise night, an additional craft or a non-human cause**. Original 1980–81 duty rosters and underlying archived files were not audited here.
