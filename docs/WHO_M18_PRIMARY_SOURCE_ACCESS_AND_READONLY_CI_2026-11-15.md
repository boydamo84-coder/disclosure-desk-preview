## Seven additional original-source reports inspected in M18

### 1. Jeremy Corbell — original CNN transcript, NOT Pentagon confirmation of exotic technology

- **Original source:** [CNN Erin Burnett OutFront, 31 May 2021, full segment transcript](https://transcripts.cnn.com/show/ebo/date/2021-05-31/segment/01). Corbell describes USS Omaha radar material, allegedly more than 14 UAP objects and footage he interprets as showing abilities beyond known technology. CNN quotes Pentagon spokeswoman confirming Navy personnel took referenced video/photos and UAP Task Force examined them. That confirmation is not the same as confirming object size/speed/origin or a water-entry claim. CNN quotes astronaut Scott Kelly's alternate optical/UAV hypothesis without proving it either.
- **Missing:** full raw Navy radar/IR, time/range calibration, source editing and an independent technical sensor reviewer. The original video of the CNN programme was not watched.

### 2. George Knapp — official seven-page House PDF, first two pages visually inspected

- **Original source:** [House Oversight original Knapp submitted written testimony, 9 Sep 2025](https://oversight.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/George-Knapp-Written-Testimony.pdf). PDF **page indexes 0 and 1** (printed pages 1 and 2) were actually rendered/visually inspected. Page 1 states Knapp is an investigative reporter **not a UFO witness**; page 2 introduces his crash-retrieval position and past Lazar/S-4 reporting. This is attributable written testimony and not validated physical recovery.
- **Missing:** remaining five source PDF pages still need page-level check, underlying original documents and independent reported-witness scrutiny.

### 3. Mick West — primary technical interpretation and source-code references

- **Original source:** [Metabunk thread, 13 Mar 2022, West original post #1](https://www.metabunk.org/threads/gimbal-ufo-a-new-analysis.12333/), also linking [his original 2022 explanatory video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qsEjV8DdSbs) and source-code/model files. His argument attributes apparent Gimbal object rotation to camera derotation, with infrared glare obscuring what might be a hot object. He explicitly says this does not identify the object or eliminate every genuine UAP possibility.
- **Missing:** independently run the model/code, raw ATFLIR sensor/camera telemetry, independent alternative model comparison and named expert methodology review.

### 4. Christopher Mellon — original signed opinion, separate from an incident metric

- **Original source:** [Mellon’s 14 Oct 2025 original Substack republication](https://christopherkmellon.substack.com/p/the-us-government-is-sitting-on-a) of his [11 Oct 2025 San Francisco Chronicle opinion](https://www.sfchronicle.com/opinion/openforum/article/uap-government-intelligence-classified-21083344.php). He argues UAP footage/records are overclassified, claims he saw excessive secrecy in prior service and asks for release. Exact extent, lawfulness and individual record decisions are not resolved by the opinion.
- **Scope:** This is a record-release/legal-policy assertion, **NOT applicable to an extraordinary-incident evidence percentage**. Original NARA transfer/FOIA and classified exception analysis remains for future research.

### 5. Avi Loeb — Chemical Geology original PDF, selected original page images read

- **Original source:** [Avi Loeb et al., Chemical Geology 670 (2024) 122415, 20-page original author PDF](https://galileo.hsites.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum6371/files/chemical_classification_of_spherules_1-s2.0-s0009254124004959-main.pdf). **PDF page indexes 0 and 10** (printed journal pages 1 and 11) were actually rendered/visually inspected. The abstract describes around 850 microspherules, 78% primitive classification and a group of D-type chemistry with some terrestrial candidate origins; page 11 shows elemental-ratio plots and sections on cosmic/igneous interpretations.
- **Evidentiary scope:** Samples were recovered from the seafloor in an area around a calculated 2014 bolide trajectory. That does **not** independently establish that the grains derive from this particular meteor, are interstellar in origin, or derive from a technological craft. The other 18 original pages, full supplementary/raw methods, samples and independent 2025 critique need specialist review.

### 6. Steven Greer — contemporaneous May 2001 reporting, not 400 verified events

- **Original source:** [ABC News original 9/10 May 2001 conference report](https://abcnews.go.com/Technology/story?id=98572&page=1), independently attributing Greer's National Press Club disclosure press conference and saying he referred to **around 400 prospective witnesses**. [WorldCat listing for original 2001 edited video](https://search.worldcat.org/title/52741633) also documents a published recording. Neither makes 400 independently verified physical cases, nor is a complete video transcript available directly in this inspection.
- **Missing:** complete unedited original press event, individual dated official source documents, witness independence/custody and alternate explanations.

### 7. James Fox — original 2022 director interview versus 2024 biography typo

- **Original director source:** [Film School Radio’s Moment of Contact original 2022 interview page](https://filmschoolradio.com/moment-of-contact-director-james-fox/), explicitly referring to the **1996** Varginha claims; the [same programme’s later The Program original director interview page](https://filmschoolradio.com/the-program-director-james-fox/) mistakenly prints **1966** in a short 2024 film biography. Do not silently shift the documented 1996 allegation date.
- **Missing:** full original documentary footage and Portuguese subtitles, independently verified official Brazil STM 1997 inquiry file and its conclusion, contemporary military/hospital records, any alleged samples with reliable custody. A filmmaker statement is not a first-person witness to a Brazilian alien recovery.
