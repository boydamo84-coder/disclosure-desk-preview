**Full description**

Explore the unexplained. Follow the evidence.

Disclosure Desk is an independent UAP and UFO news, archive and research platform. Explore current reporting, historical case dossiers, original government records and freedom of information request timelines.

- Browse sourced news and discover related reporting.
- Explore historical and newly released records, including the PURSUE catalogue.
- Read source-linked investigations and profiles of public figures involved in disclosure.
- Organise saved research links and private investigation boards.
- Use W.NE Research Edition to organise notes, citations and documents in an online research workspace.
- Explore competing explanations and research gaps rather than relying on unsupported conclusions.
- Join eligible signed-in community discussions and manage optional native news and video alerts.

Public browsing is available without an account. Some research, backup and community functions need an account and internet connection. The app includes offline archive access and local recovery tools; complete offline document authoring and cloud synchronisation are not advertised.

Disclosure Desk distinguishes published claims from verified evidence and links readers to original sources. It is independent and is not a government agency or official government news source.
