## 1. Bob Lazar — claimed December 1989 S-4 work

- **First-person historic source:** [1989 KLAS On the Record retranscription distributed by ParaNet](https://raids.org/files/mys3/lazar8.htm), archive header dated **30 Dec 1989** and broadcast listed **9 Dec 1989**. In the opening Knapp exchange, Lazar claims an S-4 job linked to EG&G/Navy and later describes gravitational propulsion. The source remains a **later third-party electronic transcript**, not the authenticated KLAS broadcast master; the archived version is search-indexed but a fresh direct open failed.
- **Independence:** Multiple web copies of the same 1989 exchange and Knapp's later repetition are **not** independent employment records or engineering tests. Background/Los Alamos references are separate and narrower than an S-4 assignment.
- **Contrary/context:** [Nevada Public Radio's original 13 Dec 2018 interview with Knapp and KLAS editor Robert Stoldal](https://www.knpr.org/show/knprs-state-of-nevada/2018-12-13/a-new-film-focuses-on-bob-lazars-area-51-story-but-is-it-for-real) discusses unconfirmed schooling and laboratory questions with disputed context. [DoD historical-review summary](https://www.defense.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/Article/3701297/dod-report-discounts-sightings-of-extraterrestrial-technology/) is an agency-wide conclusion; not an S-4-specific payroll audit.
- **Missing:** original video, signed access/employment and classified contractor records, named independent coworkers, physical samples/custody, reproducible engineering tests and second reviewer.
- **Shared source group:** `lazar-knapp-1989-s4-single-interview-chain`. **Rating: Not assessed.**

## 4. Nick Pope — 2013 conventional-explanations review of Rendlesham

- **Original authored case statement:** [Nick Pope's published Rendlesham briefing](https://nickpope.net/wpte19/rendlesham-forest/), presented as a **2013 briefing to a group of former U.S. congressional representatives**, claims his later cold-case comparison found proposed ordinary explanations unsatisfactory. Search-indexed first-party text was available; its direct URL redirected to a blocked mirror, so preservation/full article revisions require checking.
- **Correct attribution:** Pope was **not a 1980 eyewitness** and wrote an interpretation of files and later witness accounts. His essay says MoD described the reports as being of **no defence significance**, while his critique challenged that evaluation. Not finding a satisfactory conventional answer is **not a scientific measurement of an extraterrestrial craft**.
- **Independent archive context:** [The National Archives 2012 briefing, p.13](https://cdn.nationalarchives.gov.uk/documents/briefing-guide-12-07-12.pdf) confirms an actual official correspondence trail and separate nights. It is a different institution's historical record but the **same underlying Rendlesham incident**, not another independent sensor experiment. Claims of deleted/incomplete file preservation must be checked against archival custody records rather than presumed evidence of concealment.
- **Missing:** source-original 2013 presentation, exact 1980 logs, coherent dates and physical source data, Lighthouse/astronomical/radar geometry, cross-examination and second auditor.
- **Shared group:** `rendlesham-1980-distinct-nights-halt-warren-claims`. **Rating: Not assessed.**

## 5. Christopher Mellon — October 2025 classification and release claims

- **Original authored opinion:** [Mellon's 14 Oct 2025 republication](https://christopherkmellon.substack.com/p/the-us-government-is-sitting-on-a), identified as originating in the **San Francisco Chronicle, 11 Oct 2025**, paragraphs on the 2017 Navy clips, later reclassification, government records and FOIA. - **Verified context:** [DoD 27 Apr 2020 release of three Navy videos](https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/2165713/statement-by-the-department-of-defense-on-the-release-of-historical-navy-videos/) confirms those three public recordings, not an itemized proof about every suppressed record. [NARA's 24 Apr 2025 official release](https://www.archives.gov/press/press-releases/2025/nr25-07) confirms actual transfers from ODNI, DoD, FAA and NRC. Do not confuse this enacted process with any unpassed version of a later disclosure bill.
- **Competing interpretation:** Lawful national-security withholding and delayed record processing could explain some unavailable documents. Whether a *specific* withheld video was improperly classified needs its own release/classification order and independent legal review; numerical scarcity alone is insufficient.
- **Missing:** complete UAP video classification decision inventory, source/method risk evaluations, legal application per file, FOIA outcomes, 1948 film/photo chain if alleged, signed reviewer.
- **Shared group:** `mellon-2025-records-classification-policy`. **Event score: Not applicable to policy recommendation; disputed empirical overclassification details still unassessed.**
