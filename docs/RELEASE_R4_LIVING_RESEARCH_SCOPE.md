## Three approved deliverables

### Living Investigations
For a followed case or user research project, maintain a dated change log of newly discovered documents, corrections to source claims and revisions to previous assessments. Highlight *potential* implications for a prior conclusion, with source and date; never auto-change the author's original judgement. Permit dismissing noisy or duplicate notifications.

### Research Passport
Export a reproducible dossier capturing research question, scope, source inventory and identifiers, timeline, methods, comparisons, unresolved contradictions, alternative hypotheses, evidence gaps, limitations and version history. Display where a source cannot be redistributed or original material has become unavailable. Preserve proper attribution.

### W.NE professional workflow
Link approved passages and source references into the W.NE Research editor through an explicit user action. Generate structured citations/bibliography and DOCX/PDF reports with documented references, not unsupported quotations. Keep the author's manuscript and research notes private by default; draft publication requires owner approval and redaction review. No irreversible overwrites of originals or Drive backup regressions.

## Acceptance
- Version history and correction attribution are exact, not regenerated by unreviewed AI prose.
- Proper access controls and opt-in; sharing a Research Passport does not share private W.NE documents.
- DOCX/PDF export and restore tested with real embedded references, tables and images as applicable.
- News/source monitoring performs reliable deduplication; no unapproved background notifications.
- Must pass full source, browser, phone and backend security QA on final merged R4 code.
