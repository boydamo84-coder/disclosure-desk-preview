## Source access and verification boundaries

- Checked current web access to original pages for Mick West, Greer’s ABC coverage and archive, Mellon, Fox’s filmmaker interview, GPO House hearing, NARA RG 615, NASA UAP research and Kean’s original reporting.
- The official **Mike Gold PDF page 1** and the **House-filed Immaculate Constellation PDF page 1** were visually inspected in this pass. A complete page-by-page review of all listed documents **was not** repeated; follow the earlier M40–M49 source audit scopes.
- The Brazilian STM index responded **Request Rejected**; the AARO Go Fast methodology URL responded **403 Forbidden** in automated review. These exact access limits are identified in reader link titles or the prior dossier; they are not quietly treated as independently retrieved originals.
- Several mirrored records and document sources remain hosted by third parties, and some media have no verified raw/complete original. Link existence does not imply rights to republish the media.
- No source response from Pentagon, OSD or NARA is established by these source links. The two existing outreach enquiries retain their **sent, not confirmed received or answered** status.
