## Method and exact scope

This milestone examines all six people listed as the next cohort in the last verified handover. Five public statements/interpretations have specific researchable propositions and preliminary source comparisons: George Knapp, Christopher Mellon, James Fox, Nick Pope and Leslie Kean. **John Greenewald Jr. is instead documented with actual government-issued FOIA/declassification correspondence, but has no event-specific public assertion suitable for a rating in this batch.** Forcing an incident score from Greenewald's records-access work would be misleading.

Source links are original (a statement personally written, a first-party film/podcast interview, government archives, or a journal/broadcaster's original report) whenever possible. Challenges are explicitly distinguished from independent disproof. **The public existence of an interview, film, FOIA release, congressional testimony or legal complaint establishes publication/recording, not a recovered non-human craft.** This first pass is not a completed source text audit, does not show certified physical evidence and does not give personal honesty ratings. No claim is approved for numerical scoring or community voting.

## Non-negotiable evidence and publication distinctions

- **Knapp 2025:** Official House submission documents his own explicit recovery-programme assertion, including his acknowledgement that he is a journalist rather than a craft eyewitness. Government acceptance of written testimony is not government authentication of the alleged recovered material.
- **Mellon 2025:** A firsthand editorial argument about excessive classification can be tested against the status of specific Navy video releases, genuine classification-guide rules and document-transfer history. It is not automatically proof that the UAP are non-human.
- **Fox / Varginha:** Both first-person 2022 documentary reporting and the **original 1997 Superior Tribunal Militar military-police inquiry** have public records. A historical inquiry into reported military capture is not official confirmation that a biological being was recovered; the full Portuguese original and evidence chain still require expert reading.
- **Pope / Rendlesham:** UK archival material confirms reports and the MoD's security judgement. Pope's 2013 retrospective explanation and a contemporary 1980 military report are different types of evidence. UK FOI decisions about his job records are neither proof nor disproof of a craft landing.
- **Kean / Grusch:** Reporters described multiple intelligence source accounts, some confidential. The initial June 2023 story, Grusch's later congressional testimony and other interviews repeat **overlapping underlying allegations**; they must not be tallied as independent physical recovery demonstrations. AARO's broader March 2024 review is a contrary institutional position, not necessarily an exhaustive examination of every confidential source.
- **Greenewald:** Government letterheads, FOIA tracking numbers and partial releases verify that records were requested and handled; **not** the extraordinary events being investigated.
- **Source limitations:** The Brazil military PDF and ODNI archival log are linkable original-document citations, but some automated document viewers rejected screenshots; preserve the archive finding aid and record this accessibility limitation. Do not imply every page or translation was verified simply because a search result supplies parsed text.
