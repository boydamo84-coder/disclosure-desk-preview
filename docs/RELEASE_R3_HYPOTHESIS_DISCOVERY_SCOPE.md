# DD-WEB-R3 — Hypothesis & Discovery (queued)

**Initial status:** APPROVED AS ROADMAP, NOT BUILT, NOT DEPLOYABLE.
**Target branch:** `queue/dd-web-r3-hypothesis-discovery`
**Depends on:** DD-WEB-R2 deployed, stable, accepted; rebase on current verified live production code.

## Two approved deliverables

### Hypothesis Lab
Provide a structured, private-by-default research worksheet for multiple explanations of the same case. Include explicit assumptions, predicted observations, potential disconfirming evidence, alternative interpretations and what data could test each hypothesis. Support versioned, timestamped hypotheses so new evidence does not silently rewrite earlier predictions. Never label a probability of alien origin or fabricate objective numerical certainty.

### Research Gap Finder
Across source-linked cases and citations actually accessible to the application, surface research needs: missing original date or precise time, unverified primary provenance, absent instrument data or metadata, uncertain location, untraceable witness independence, inaccessible original document, or contradictory archive descriptions. Distinguish “not supplied in this archive” from “does not exist.” Summaries should link straight to the relevant original case/evidence item and allow human confirmation.

## Workflow and privacy
- Integrate with Case Connections and R2 provenance graph; suggestions are not proof.
- Each suggested gap has a source-based reason and possible next research action.
- Keep project hypothesis boards private unless the owner deliberately publishes a reviewed version.
- Do not solicit exact witness coordinates or confidential information as a default.
- Show uncertainty and data provenance across web, Android and W.NE.

## Acceptance
- Tests cover uncertain/incomplete source data and prevent fabricated evidence claims.
- Attributed, explainable results with understandable missing-data labels.
- Source preservation, opt-in, research project isolation and no user-data leakage.
- Real usability tests at phone and desktop sizes plus independent final production SHA approval.
