# Disclosure Desk M101 private Android acceptance build

M101 packages Issue #22 from draft PR #23. It is version 0.16.3, build 37, and installs as **Disclosure Desk Beta M101** alongside M100 and the live app. The new beta starts with its own app storage. Keep the earlier installation if you have unsaved or unexported local research.

The build includes the SVG dock and More menu, source-linked research updates, simplified News and Who’s Who, new public research permalinks and the weekly Spotlight question. Android Back closes More before moving to a previous screen. Community publishing and uploads remain disabled.

On the Galaxy S24 Ultra, check More and Android Back from Home and a secondary page, the banner cog, News paging, bundled dossiers and FOIA links, the W.NE save and return flow, large fonts and TalkBack. Check any account-specific behaviour with approved test data. Keep M100 installed while comparing the two builds.

The review workflow creates a debug-signed APK only. Production, Play, and editorial sign-off remain separate.
