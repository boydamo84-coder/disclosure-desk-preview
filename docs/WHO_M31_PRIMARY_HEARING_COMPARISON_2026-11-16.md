## 2023: three official prepared statements (individually attributed, not three independent sensor confirmations)

### David Fravor — direct pilot view versus *reported* radar and later crew footage
- **Written original:** [Fravor statement, three pages](https://docs.house.gov/meetings/GO/GO06/20230726/116282/HHRG-118-GO06-Wstate-FravorD-20230726.pdf), **PDF pp. 1–2**; **oral:** 2023 GPO transcript, **lines 822–906**.
- Fravor describes his own visual 2004 observation of a white Tic Tac-shaped object and a separate report from the Princeton controller that a radar contact appeared at a CAP point approximately 60 miles away. He says **another aircrew subsequently recorded** the ~90-second infrared video, not his own flight. Those are three different evidence lineages: visual, controller-reported radar, later crew video.
- **Gap:** The original Princeton radar track, raw infrared file/metadata, matching clocks and independent measurement reconstruction were not supplied by the House statement. His verbal speed inference is a reported inference, not instrument data independently analysed here.

### Ryan Graves — squadron sensor experience versus **other pilots'** near miss
- **Written original:** [Graves statement, five pages](https://docs.house.gov/meetings/GO/GO06/20230726/116282/HHRG-118-GO06-Wstate-GravesR-20230726.pdf), **PDF pp. 1–3**; **oral:** 2023 GPO transcript, **lines 656–744**.
- Graves describes personally encountering unexplained radar/IR tracks, and describes a cube-in-sphere near miss as an incident **involving other pilots** rather than something he personally flew through. His account of the Gallaudet safety email was attributed to Gallaudet, and does not represent a second independently produced email.
- **Gap:** No raw squadron sensor exports, original 2014 near-miss crew testimony or safety reporting log were inspected. Do not mix his W-72 incidents with the individual Navy *Go Fast* video.

### David Grusch — first-person official reporting, secondhand retrieval allegation
- **Written original:** [Grusch statement, three pages](https://docs.house.gov/meetings/GO/GO06/20230726/116282/HHRG-118-GO06-Wstate-GruschD-20230726.pdf), **PDF pp. 1–2**; **oral:** 2023 GPO transcript, **lines 746–822**.
- His statement records personal official assignments and reports he made, **but says information about the alleged multi-decade retrieval/reverse-engineering programme came from other people**, and that he had been denied the additional access. Both sources make that distinction.
- **Gap:** The public House statements do not grant access to the claimed recovered objects, private records, classified source testimony or independent programme inspections. Attribution of the allegation is **not** factual proof of a recovered craft.

## 2024: four original prepared statements, and correct attribution for two addenda

### Tim Gallaudet — first-person received email, inference about its disappearance
- **Written original:** [Gallaudet statement, seven pages](https://docs.house.gov/meetings/GO/GO12/20241113/117721/HHRG-118-GO12-Wstate-GallaudetPhDRearAdmiralUSNavyRetT-20241113.pdf), **PDF pp. 1–2**; **oral:** 2024 GPO transcript, **lines 685–800**.
- Gallaudet states he received a secure 2015 safety-of-flight email attaching a Navy infrared video, later not found in his account. His view that it was moved into a special access programme is **his inference**; the original email and removal audit logs were not provided.
- **Critical distinction:** The **Go Fast video** is a specific publicly studied object, not all W-72 near-miss reports. Its flight performance has a later relevant official technical analysis below.

### Luis Elizondo — assertions about programmes, not released independent proof
- **Written original:** [Elizondo statement, three pages](https://docs.house.gov/meetings/GO/GO12/20241113/117721/HHRG-118-GO12-Wstate-ElizondoL-20241113.pdf), **PDF pp. 1–2**; **oral:** 2024 GPO transcript, **lines 800–909**.
- The text attributes claims concerning advanced UAP technology, secret programmes and potential oversight problems to Elizondo. The prepared document does not itself disclose separately verifiable physical technological samples, complete raw sensor data or classified records.
- **Important addendum metadata trap:** The House Repository lists *Testimony Addendum #1* under Elizondo, but the [two-page uploaded PDF](https://docs.house.gov/meetings/GO/GO12/20241113/117721/HHRG-118-GO12-Wstate-ElizondoL-20241113-SD001.pdf) is explicitly titled **Written Statement of Matthew Ford**, dated **18 November 2024**, and was added to the House index **20 November 2024**. It contains Ford's journalism and allegations, **not a second signed Elizondo statement**. File the attachment under Elizondo's repository group only, but **credit the content to Ford**.

### Michael Shellenberger — named allegation based on reported source
- **Written original:** [Shellenberger submission, 212 pages including lengthy attachments](https://docs.house.gov/meetings/GO/GO12/20241113/117721/HHRG-118-GO12-Wstate-ShellenbergerM-20241113.pdf), **PDF pp. 1–5 sampled**; **oral:** 2024 GPO transcript, **lines 909–1040**.
- The original testimony attributes the **Immaculate Constellation** allegation to a purported unnamed government source and a report said to be shared with congressional committees. - **Gap:** 212 pages were **not** fully checked in sequence. Confidential source origins, the purported programme database, alleged imagery and audit trails remain unavailable; treat source-independence and existence as unresolved.

### Mike Gold — science and policy proposal, *not* a physical-incident claim
- **Written original:** [Gold statement, five pages](https://docs.house.gov/meetings/GO/GO12/20241113/117721/HHRG-118-GO12-Wstate-GoldM-20241113.pdf), **PDF pp. 1–3**; **oral:** 2024 GPO transcript, **lines 1040–1128**.

## Direct contrary evidence and scope — include both sides, without false equivalence

1. **2024 Department of Defense/AARO institutional historic review:** [Original DoD press statement, 8 March 2024](https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/3700894/statement-by-pentagon-press-secretary-maj-gen-pat-ryder-on-the-historical-recor/). AARO reports finding **no verifiable evidence** of US government/private extraterrestrial technology recovery or reverse engineering, and says investigated alleged programmes either did not exist, were real programmes misidentified as alien-related, or were discontinued. This is important **contrary institutional evidence**, but the public statement itself does not expose every confidential witness record or case-specific chain. Also, the March 2024 report **predates** Shellenberger's November 2024 named Immaculate Constellation dossier; do not present the earlier report as direct examination of his later materials.
2. **2025 AARO Go Fast case-specific analysis:** [Official Go Fast technical report, dated 6 February 2025](https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/case_resolution_reports/AARO_GoFast_Case_Resolution_Card_Methodology_Final.pdf), **PDF pp. 1–5 and methodological appendix**. AARO says the object's identity was not established, but the imagery indicates **no anomalous speed**, with wind-adjusted 5–92 mph estimates under its modeled heading scenarios. Crucially, AARO acknowledges it used the publicly released compressed clip because original data and complete sensor metadata were unavailable; precise aircraft heading/location were also unknown. It is a **case-specific competing analysis of the *Go Fast* footage**, not an explanation of Gallaudet's email-removal assertion or of Fravor's distinct 2004 sighting.
