**Prepared 15 November 2026 | isolated editorial branch | PROVISIONAL, NOT published fact-checks or numeric ratings**

## Scope and publication discipline

This checkpoint expands **five of the 17 professional/public-work profiles** with a narrowly attributed, directly inspectable claim review. It **does not delete** their original-publication readers. With the seven existing 2023/2024 hearing records, the directory now contains **12 claim/recommendation drafts across 12 people and 44 provisional item-level evidence references** (original 27 plus 17). The 27 NASA/Congress source-reader role panels and 17 original-publication readers remain in place: all 50 unique people continue to have a source reader.

Every new `claimEvidence` is `source_review_pending`, with `researchEvidence.status='research_draft'`. **No 0–100 site score is approved; no actual user vote or backend voting is available**. Editorial source counts are not independent physical corroborations. Claims about alleged craft are not treated as proven from the existence of an interview or official government report. Claims about methods or published analyses are assessed as particular testable propositions, *not* a scientist's or skeptic's personal honesty, scientific worth, or whole worldview. All entries still require independent fact-checking, original media/source availability checks, rights review and a named second editor before scoring.

## 1. Bob Lazar — December 1989 S-4 account

**Review target:** Lazar publicly described being hired to a site he called S-4 and working with alleged recovered propulsion systems. Historical claim attribution is established by records of the interview; the work assignment and underlying physical technology are not confirmed in this ledger.

- Original 9 December 1989 KLAS `On the Record` transcript preserved by ParaNet: https://raids.org/files/mys3/lazar8.htm — locator: opening Knapp/Lazar exchange, S-4 employment answer. This is a historical transcript *reproduction*; original broadcaster tape identity/transcription accuracy should be matched independently before citing exact words.
- Third-party archive index of earliest interview recordings, including May/November/December 1989: https://www.papooselake.org/interview-transcripts — locator: 1989 broadcast index. This reproduces claim publication, not S-4 employment.
- 13 December 2018 **Nevada Public Radio** reporter/editor retrospective: https://www.knpr.org/show/knprs-state-of-nevada/2018-12-13/a-new-film-focuses-on-bob-lazars-area-51-story-but-is-it-for-real — locator: KNPR discussion of MIT, Los Alamos and Knapp's checks. Records unresolved education/official employment responses alongside Knapp's own observation of people recognising Lazar at Los Alamos. None is original S-4 payroll or hardware.
- 8 March 2024 **DoD/AARO** general programme review summary: https://www.defense.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/Article/3701297/dod-report-discounts-sightings-of-extraterrestrial-technology/ — locator: historical recovered-technology review, not a personnel-level examination of Lazar.

**Unresolved before any score:** authentic 1989 broadcaster masters, site/work orders, independently verifiable technical samples, employment and academic files, independent verification of the alleged site and programme. Explicitly distinguish Los Alamos association from S-4 craft allegation; neither an allegation's history nor independent doubts about the claimant settles the physical claim.

## 2. Jake Barber — January 2025 alleged egg-shaped object retrieval

**Review target:** Barber said he was involved in retrieving an egg-shaped object. An interview can confirm attribution, but camera footage, editorial narration and syndicated news are not independent custody evidence.

- Original **NewsNation** interview segment: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PGOpHdHSjTE — locator: 18 January 2025 publisher clip. It preserves the public account; unedited camera original, capture conditions and source identity must be separately authenticated.
- **Yahoo** redistribution of the broadcaster's news coverage: https://www.yahoo.com/news/air-force-veteran-whistleblower-claims-232514734.html — locator: original NewsNation reporting, January 2025; the article is syndication, **not** a second independent source.
- 8 March 2024 **DoD/AARO** historical-programme assessment: https://www.defense.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/Article/3701297/dod-report-discounts-sightings-of-extraterrestrial-technology/ — locator: prior institutional historical scope. It *predates the January 2025 broadcast* and cannot specifically rule on Barber's later allegations.

**Unresolved before any score:** original files and timestamps, filming chain, retrieval team and helicopter records, independent source testimony, physical sample access and reproducible lab testing. Current entry neither verifies nor disproves a recovered non-human object.

- Warren's first-person **NewsNation Reality Check** appearance: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=21yW_XZR2LU — locator: 19 August 2025; Warren approximately 08:47–32:40. Original interview evidence of the assertion; retrospectively recorded.

**Unresolved before any score:** authentic 1980 personnel rosters, logbooks, site coordinates, contemporary witness statements and side-by-side timeline of different nights. Avoid rhetorical attacks, conflating participants, or promoting a later book as contemporary evidence.

## 4. Mick West — 2022 Gimbal camera-glare hypothesis

**Review target:** West's technically testable proposal that rotation shown in a January 2015 Navy image can arise from optics/camera motion. This **does not claim the object is definitively identified** or invalidate independent aviator reports.

- Original **Metabunk** March 13 2022 model and explanations: https://www.metabunk.org/threads/gimbal-ufo-a-new-analysis.12333/ — locator: opening Gimbal reconstruction, camera angle and infrared glare argument, video/simulation references. Original analyst work, not raw aircraft telemetry.
- **DoD** 27 April 2020 source video release statement: https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/release/article/2165713/statement-by-the-department-of-defense-on-the-release-of-historical-navy-videos/ — locator: January 2015 Navy imagery. Confirms video provenance as described by DoD; neither optical explanation nor exceptional flight proved by government authentication.
- **Metabunk** 2026 technical counterargument discussion: https://www.metabunk.org/threads/the-gimbal-ufo-marik-claims-new-findings-falsify-prosaic-explanations.14839/ — locator: early-frame rotation/downward pan issues and competing models. A forum debate contains testable criticisms but is not original calibrated sensor data or a settled lab report.

**Unresolved before any score:** original sensor/mission metadata, reproducible frame-tracking, alternative target geometry, camera hardware detail and independent attempts to falsify each hypothesis. Any future score concerns this *one model's evidential support*, never an overall 'debunker believability' judgement.

## 5. Avi Loeb — IM1 seafloor spherule composition interpretation

**Review target:** Loeb and collaborators' peer-reviewed published chemical classifications of sampled spherules, compared with a subsequent journal re-evaluation. The sample chemistry is a different question from a proven trajectory match, extrasolar origin or **alien manufacture**, none of which is established as a single proposition here.

- **Loeb et al., Chemical Geology** research article, 2024, volume 670, article 122415: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0009254124004959 — locator: 2023 expedition, element analysis, classification and interpretation. Accepted 16 September 2024; available online 18 September 2024, journal volume dated December 2024.
- **Chemical Geology** opposing peer-reviewed re-evaluation, 2025, volume 677, article 122628: https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S000925412500018X — locator: volcanic origin argument for many samples, including contested D-type classifications. It is a meaningful scientific challenge to the interpretation, not a character judgement.
- Original **Harvard-hosted Galileo Project** publication notice: https://galileo.hsites.harvard.edu/news/new-paper-chemical-classification-im1-spherules-published-chemical-geology — locator: 23 September 2024. It represents the research team's own account, not independent replication.

**Unresolved before any score:** physical sample provenance and contamination, independent reanalysis of chemistry, particle taxonomy, background/control sampling, source-bolide position/dispersion, geochemical uncertainty and whether any result can distinguish natural from manufactured materials. No assertion of extraterrestrial technology is supported by these three links.
