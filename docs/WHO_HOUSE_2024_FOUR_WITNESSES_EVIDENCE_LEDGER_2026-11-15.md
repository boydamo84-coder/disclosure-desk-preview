# Who's Who — the 2024 congressional hearing: four source-first research trails

**15 November 2026 · isolated GitHub editorial-development branch · DRAFT · NO site ratings or community voting**

## Scope and method

The House held `Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena: Exposing the Truth` on **13 November 2024**. All four named hearing witnesses already exist among Disclosure Desk's original 50 public figures. This milestone adds one individually attributed public statement or scientific-policy recommendation per person, with **four public source/context records per statement (16 new records)**. The three 2023 witness trails remain unchanged; combined staged count is **7 profiles / 27 provisional research records**.

Sources readers can open:
- Original **Government Publishing Office** complete hearing record, **Serial 118-135**: https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-118hhrg57440/html/CHRG-118hhrg57440.htm (oral statements printed starting pages 9, 11, 13, 16 respectively).
- Original **US House committee repository**: https://docs.house.gov/Committee/Calendar/ByEvent.aspx?EventId=117722 (individual written statements, addenda and submitted documents).
- Original House hearing announcement and testimony links: https://oversight.house.gov/hearing/unidentified-anomalous-phenomena-exposing-the-truth/

**Provenance rule:** A hearing record authenticates what was publicly said. It does not authenticate an event, classified source, anonymous whistleblower, recovered craft or extraordinary interpretation. A second copy of the same witness's prepared remarks is **not independent corroboration**.

## 1. Tim Gallaudet — 2015 Navy safety email and Go Fast

**Attribution, not an adjudicated incident:** In his Nov 2024 testimony Gallaudet said he received a January 2015 Navy email warning of an aviation risk with the Go Fast footage attached, and that this email disappeared from his account.

- **Original oral statement:** GPO record, `STATEMENT OF TIM GALLAUDET` (printed page 9; search `URGENT SAFETY OF FLIGHT ISSUE`), https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-118hhrg57440/html/CHRG-118hhrg57440.htm . It records his firsthand description of receiving the email and his inference of compartmentation. The original email, server logs, recipient confirmations and investigation outcome are **not** in the public record audited here.
- **Original prepared written testimony:** https://www.congress.gov/118/meeting/house/117721/witnesses/HHRG-118-GO12-Wstate-GallaudetPhDRearAdmiralUSNavyRetT-20241113.pdf . It has longer details and the author's interpretation. It remains the same witness's account.
- **Official counteranalysis for the attached *video*, not the email:** AARO's **6 February 2025** Go Fast case-resolution card/methodology: https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/case_resolution_reports/AARO_GoFast_Case_Resolution_Card_Methodology_Final.pdf . On page 1 it assesses altitude around **13,000 ft** and wind-adjusted speed **5–92 mph**; page 3 discusses **motion parallax**. AARO was unable to definitively identify the object but found no anomalous flight performance. The original sensor file/metadata were not available to AARO, an uncertainty to document. These findings do **not** adjudicate whether the email was received or deleted and do **not** resolve every separate aircrew report.
- **Authentic footage versus interpreted capabilities:** Department of Defense **27 April 2020** release of three Navy videos: https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/2165713/statement-by-the-department-of-defense-on-the-release-of-historical-navy-videos/ . Original Navy video status is not scientific confirmation of an extraordinary object.

**Open checks:** Seek the original message and IT audit trail, independently confirmed recipients and safety incident logs; compare the aircraft geometry and missing raw metadata to AARO's published calculation; distinguish the video from the reported near misses and the claim of concealment. No number cleared.

## 2. Luis Elizondo — advanced UAP technology and purported secret programmes

**Attribution:** In November 2024 Elizondo publicly asserted the US and some other governments possessed advanced UAP technology and that certain UAP-related programmes lacked appropriate oversight. These are *his claims*, not established facts from this research set.

- **Original oral statement:** GPO transcript, printed page 11, `STATEMENT OF LUIS ELIZONDO`, https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-118hhrg57440/html/CHRG-118hhrg57440.htm . The public text includes his allegations; it is not an actual recovered sample, official programme record or laboratory result.
- **Original written statement:** https://oversight.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Written-Testimony-Elizondo.pdf ; opening paragraphs describe his allegations and his personal experience. The written and oral versions are one witness, not independent confirmation of each other.
- **Official alternative/institutional assessment:** DoD summary of AARO's historical report (8 March 2024): https://www.defense.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/Article/3701297/dod-report-discounts-sightings-of-extraterrestrial-technology/ . AARO reported no verifiable evidence of government or private extraterrestrial technology. This *predates* Elizondo's November testimony; it does not prove that all subsequently made or confidential assertions were investigated.
- **Filed witness/addendum record:** https://docs.house.gov/Committee/Calendar/ByEvent.aspx?EventId=117722 ; Elizondo's addendum was separately listed, but it is not automatically independent corroboration of his assertion.

**Open checks:** Identify inspectable original programme materials and independently verifiable technical results; obtain authorised chain of custody; investigate each alleged oversight breach separately; compare AARO's actual investigated scope against the precise allegation rather than reducing either to a person-level honesty score. No number cleared.

## 3. Michael Shellenberger — the alleged “Immaculate Constellation” programme

**Attribution:** He said he obtained information from sources concerning an alleged unacknowledged government UAP intelligence programme. The public hearing record confirms the allegations were submitted, **not** that the programme exists.

- **Original oral statement:** GPO transcript printed page 13, `STATEMENT OF MICHAEL SHELLENBERGER`, https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-118hhrg57440/html/CHRG-118hhrg57440.htm .
- **Original written testimony:** https://oversight.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Written-Testimony-Shellenberger.pdf . This quotes DoD spokesperson Sue Gough denying records of any special access programme with that name, alongside the reporting and statements from unnamed sources. **Important:** the PDF cover says **13 November 2023**, whereas the House hearing archive and transcript say **13 November 2024**. Treat it as a *source-internal date discrepancy*, not evidence of a 2023 hearing. The DoD quote is embedded in Shellenberger's submission; don't call this quotation an independently retrieved DoD original statement.
- **Actual submitted allegation document:** US House repository, 12-page `Immaculate Constellation` submitted report, https://docs.house.gov/meetings/GO/GO06/20241113/117722/HHRG-118-GO06-20241113-SD003.pdf . The opening section states the allegations and claims access to non-public records. Public filing is evidence of **submission/publication**, not a certification of authorship, access, authenticity, correct programme name, or underlying surveillance.
- **General institutional competing assessment:** 8 March 2024 DoD/AARO historical report summary: https://www.defense.gov/News/News-Stories/Article/Article/3701297/dod-report-discounts-sightings-of-extraterrestrial-technology/ . It predates this hearing and **must not** be represented as a programme-name-specific assessment. The most directly relevant quoted denial above is contained in the witness's own written testimony.

**Open checks:** Verify actual records of the named programme or agency-specific authorisation; identify truly independent sources and non-circular corroboration without outing protected witnesses; examine original imagery/sensor metadata and how they reached the report; document responsive DoD statements with original release chain; investigate the PDF date error. No number cleared.

## 4. Mike Gold — research proposals, *not* an extraordinary event claim

**Attribution:** Gold advocated reducing scientific stigma, using quality data, studying NASA archives, considering civilian-reporting pathways, and developing analytical tools or dedicated UAP instruments.

- **Original oral statement:** GPO printed page 16, `STATEMENT OF MICHAEL GOLD`, https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-118hhrg57440/html/CHRG-118hhrg57440.htm . He explicitly says he is speaking **for himself**, not NASA, Redwire or another organisation.
- **Original written recommendations:** https://oversight.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Written-Testimony-Gold.pdf , especially Sections III–IV, possible NASA research programmes and dedicated instruments. This is a *proposal*, not a claim of recovered non-human technology.
- **Institutional context:** NASA UAP Independent Study and final report landing page, https://science.nasa.gov/uap/ , documenting its 14 September 2023 report.
- **Actual prior team membership:** NASA 21 October 2022 roster, https://www.nasa.gov/general/nasa-announces-unidentified-aerial-phenomena-study-team-members/ . This verifies membership but does **not** make his later views official NASA policy.

**Important design safeguard implemented:** the draft has `claimKind: "policy_recommendation"`. Its editorial and community rating cards display **Not applicable**, never `0/100` or a voting slider, even if a mistaken future editor attaches an otherwise fully populated `review.status: published`. Sources remain readable. This distinction can later be reused for legislative proposals, scientific methods and other role/policy profiles.
