For each claim, start at the full public [Who’s Who profile](https://uapdisclosuredesk.com/who-is-who.html?person=david-fravor) (that particular live URL is an example of navigation only; **the M29 source panels are NOT live yet**) or the repository's staged `data/public-people-directory.json`. Never treat a count of news links as independent eyewitness corroboration.

For detailed steps and the required blank signoff form, consult [M29 editorial human-review protocol](https://github.com/boydamo84-coder/disclosure-desk-preview/blob/prep/who-evidence-m29-editorial-gates-20261116/docs/WHO_M29_EDITORIAL_HUMAN_REVIEW_PROTOCOL_2026-11-16.md). A later authorised release requires a properly obtained first- and second-human-editor record, source-specific contrary and original evidence, owner approval, privacy/portrait rights and a separate authenticated community voting security review. A `null` or missing confirmation is **not zero corroboration**; it means no independent confirmation count has been established or approved.
