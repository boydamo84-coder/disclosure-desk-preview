# Disclosure Desk — Approved post-launch Research Roadmap

**Planning only. These are not built or live yet.**

## Phase A — Case Intelligence: `Investigate` any case
- Case page invokes read-only intelligence assembly: primary source/attribution, timeline, map region, explicitly similar historical cases, related articles, clips and potential duplicates.
- Distinguish verified relationships from algorithmic suggestions; label uncertain associations. Link to existing W.NE Research and Evidence Connections without exposing private Boards.
- Acceptance: trustworthy source URLs, date/time ordering, understandable duplicate warnings, no fabricated matches, source provenance attached, graceful no-results state.

## Phase B — What was in the sky?
- Researcher-controlled investigation tools for date, approximate city/region and time zone; possible aircraft, satellite/Starlink, meteor/astronomical, weather and space-launch explanations.
- Historical ADS-B coverage can be incomplete, satellite orbit propagation has temporal limits, public data may have gaps and time-zone ambiguity. State these limits instead of automatically classifying sightings as explained.
- Acceptance: external-source citations, timestamp precision/caveats, UTC/local display, responsible uncertainty indicators, no GPS leakage, never infer 'not extraterrestrial' from a possible match.

## Phase C — Independent witness matching
- Suggest corroborating sightings by *approximate town/region and time window* without revealing home addresses, personal contact details, device metadata, private photo EXIF or exact location.
- Verify independence: shared social/media origin, duplicate wording, reposts and coordinated submissions reduce confidence. Require user opt-in before contacting/connecting witnesses.
- Acceptance: town/city only, anti-harassment controls, duplicate detection, false-match explanations, moderation, and no public eyewitness identity by default.

## Phase D — Evidence quality and case status
- Provide a sourced checklist for original files, capture date/time, chain of custody, metadata, independent witnesses, sensor calibration, competing hypotheses, corrections and changes over time.
- Separate evidence **completeness** from *strangeness* and from any extraordinary claim's plausibility. Don't use an 'alien probability' score.
- Acceptance: clear missing-evidence flags, externally traceable sources, visible change history, distinguish confirmed observation vs interpretation and researcher hypotheses.

## Phase E — Publish an Investigation
- Convert a W.NE research project into an owner-reviewed case-study artifact with evidence citations, factual chronology, limitations, competing explanations and a persistent case-study URL.
- Publication **opt-in only**: private draft by default, preview redaction, explicit Publish confirmation, version history, correction/retraction, safe export PDF/DOCX with references.
- Acceptance: creator ownership, private notes and Boards never leak, deleted/retracted content appropriately inaccessible, citations and revision history preserved, community reports can be flagged; publication does not assert official verification.

## Product principles
1. Source-first, independent and nonpartisan; clear distinction between claim, observation and evidence.
2. Respect uncertainty, including cases without sufficient evidence.
3. Protect witnesses: city-level public locations, signed-in submissions, anti-abuse flags and self-moderation.
4. No background location collection, no forced cloud and no private research publishing by accident.
5. Accessible mobile-first design; use current 11-tab navigation rather than add five more tabs.
6. Phase incrementally and feature-gate; evaluate usage and researcher feedback before paying for external aviation/astronomy feeds.
