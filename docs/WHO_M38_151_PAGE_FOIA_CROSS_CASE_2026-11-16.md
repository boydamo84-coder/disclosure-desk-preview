## Main finding: 151 pages in two *different* contexts

We have discovered another apparently promising **151-page** OSD release, but **it is not established to be the seven-document referral we are tracking**. This matters because news articles, PDF covers, and numerical coincidence can make distinct FOIA sets appear identical.

### A. Navy `DON-NAVY-2020-007231`: *seven documents totaling 151 pages referred out*

[Original government-authored NAVAIR decision letter, mirrored by the requester](https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/navy/2020-007231.pdf), **dated 8 July 2025**, request dated **8 April 2020**. We re-inspected the original PDF cover and second page visually (zero-based pages **0 and 1**). The letter explicitly distinguishes:
- **4 email groups, 34 pages** released with redactions;
- **59 emails and 3 legal memoranda, 498 pages** withheld in full;
- **7 documents, 151 pages** referred to the **OSD FOIA coordinator** for its own review and *direct response* to the original requester.

**Newly verified M38 detail:** the **second page** provides a referral-specific Pentagon mailbox: `osd.mc-alex.oatsd-pclt.mbx.pcld-correspondence@mail.mil`. This is an address printed in the authentic **July 2025 Navy letter**, not an independently verified *current November 2026* mailbox. The [OSD/JS FOIA Requester Service Center website](https://www.esd.whs.mil/FOID/Contact-Us/) separately publishes `whs.mc-alex.esd.mbx.osd-js-foia-requester-service-center@mail.mil` as its office mailbox (index available but direct page access returned 403). These should not be conflated with a demonstrated assigned action officer.

[Black Vault reporting dated 9 July 2025](https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/navy-withheld-nearly-500-pages-about-uap-video-release-decision-records-show-foia-pressure-drove-disclosure/) described the OSD referral as **pending at that time** and said the requester appealed the Navy's full withholdings. As of the **16 November 2026 public-source searches performed here**, **we have not located a case-specific OSD referral number, disposition, or confirmed released set for those seven documents**. This is **NOT proof** the referral is still pending, has been withheld or has been released. The outcome of the Navy appeal also was not verified.

### B. OSD/JS `19-F-1420`: another **151-page reporting label**, but a different original request

The [published OSD/JS 1 July 2025 reply, case `19-F-1420`](https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/osd/19-F-1420.pdf) replies to a request made **25 June 2019**, before the Navy's April 2020 request, concerning Pentagon public-affairs correspondence on UAP-video questions.

Its **own government-authored cover letter** explicitly identifies **154 pages responsive**. [The Black Vault's July 15, 2025 article and attached download](https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/do-not-respond-pentagon-staff-instructed-to-ignore-the-black-vaults-uap-inquiry/) describe **151 pages released** in prose but label the downloadable PDF **156 pages**. A [5 November 2026 secondary review](https://eyesonlyarchive.com/2026/11/foia-case-19-f-1420-the-pentagon-email-that-answered-its-own-question/) independently points out the **151/154/156 counting mismatch**, but is **not an independent government release or explanation**. We do not assert these three numbers are interchangeable or that the mismatch has been resolved. Response pages, redacted released pages and container pages can differ, but a specific explanation was not authenticated.

The case's reported **19 August 2019** internal email refers to a staff instruction to forward a question to Public Affairs while stating that a Navy/DOPSR response had not been supplied. This is an **attributed administrative correspondence account**, not case-specific video footage or aircraft radar. Be careful not to generalise the 2019 disposition to later **27 April 2020 DoD-authorised public release** or imply the internal administrative handling established anything extraordinary about object movement.

**Access limitation:** the `19-F-1420` PDF was accessible through a web-extracted text index, including the original first-letter date and printed 154-page scope. The web screenshot tool **rejected the approximately 19.9 MB PDF** owing to a file-size limit, and no local original PDF bytes/hashes were obtained. The original letter's first page was **not visually checked in M38**, unlike the Navy letter's two page images. Treat page-count data as an agency text statement and site download label, not a reproduced local page-by-page forensic count.

**Most important:** `19-F-1420` has a distinct **June 2019** request, a **July 1 2025** final letter, its own media count discrepancy, and **no located official document confirming that its responsive records are the later seven documents referred under Navy `DON-NAVY-2020-007231`**. A coincidence of published **151** numbers is **not** an identity graph edge. It is a potentially *misleading near-match*, not the answer we were seeking.

That does not establish either the seven-document referral's disposition or the contents of case `19-F-1420`. ## National Archives: historical 1988 report remains without government-hosted version

No directly **NARA-hosted May 9, 1988 revised Reference Report on MJ-12** was located in targeted `archives.gov` and Reagan Library source searches during M38. M36 had **visually examined** a historical third-party scan of the **1987 item 9 “1954”** beside the May 1988 underlined **“1956”**, and NARA's [official online MJ-12 reference page](https://www.archives.gov/research/military/air-force/ufos) prints **1956**. This supports a clear *document-version pathway*, **not a conclusive official custody authentication** of the private 1988 scan and certainly not verification of the purported MJ-12 committee.

Official [National Archives College Park researcher guidance](https://www.archives.gov/college-park/researcher-info) lists `archives2reference@nara.gov` as a textual reference contact. M37 already recorded the [Reagan Library's official MJ-12 topic guide](https://www.reaganlibrary.gov/public/archives/textual/topics/operamaj.pdf) with WHORM references and Keyworth **RAC Box 16** folders; a finding aid **does not mean any listed folder actually contains the May 1988 revised reference report**.
