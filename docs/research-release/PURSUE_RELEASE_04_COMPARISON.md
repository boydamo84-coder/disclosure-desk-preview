# PURSUE Release 04 comparison

26 November 2026 · Disclosure Desk internal release review

The provisional sample contains ten government-authored PDFs. Each identifier matched one Release 04 row in the government catalogue snapshot and one listing in the [UFO Declassified topic index](https://ufo-declassified.com/topics/pursue-release-04/). Release 04 was dated 10 July 2026. We downloaded the ten government-authored scans linked from the comparison listings, inspected the first two pages using extracted text or OCR and verified the PDF page counts. Direct requests to War.gov returned HTTP 403 from this environment. The exact byte comparison with War.gov remains open.

The government CSV used for this review has SHA-256 `bae7455d6b5dff3ab84beb22607bc119db27cfb45a2746cec3c39e8a7662b247`. It matches the published verification for the [21 November catalogue capture](https://www.pursueindex.com/methodology/). Original War.gov URLs come from the catalogue. The original catalogue incorporates occasional repeated identifiers, so comparisons use the release, identifier and URL together.

All ten were present in the two catalogues. Differences in editorial titles, date precision and named locations are useful search metadata. The three Range Fouler reports share a form and some common language. Their record IDs, associated videos and reported dates distinguish them.

The existing archive entries and older Navy video cases have their own identifiers. Case-level duplicate status across external archives remains unverified.
