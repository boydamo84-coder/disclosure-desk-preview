# Who's Who Milestone 11 — original-record comparison for seven 2023–2024 congressional hearing claims

**Date:** 15 November 2026 · **Status:** first-pass, unsigned research; no numerical or community rating · **Location:** isolated GitHub feature branch only

## Scope of this research

This stage does **not** purport to independently verify UAP origins. It checks what seven specific people actually said in the public original House hearing transcripts, identifies repeated sources and genuinely independent first-person perspectives where possible, compares relevant official or primary reporting, and identifies which claims still cannot be corroborated using the sources available to readers.

The 23 original claim/recommendation research drafts remain. Only these **seven** now have an additional, explicitly labelled **original-record comparison draft**; the other 16 retain prior provisional source catalogues. Zero second-editor signoffs, public 0–100 scores and community ballots have been approved. A hearing being sworn and officially filed confirms testimony; it does not itself verify alleged recovered non-human physical material, classified programme contents, speeds or device manufacturing.

### Primary public documents consulted

1. **2023 US House hearing** — [official GovInfo original transcript (26 July 2023)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-118hhrg53022/html/CHRG-118hhrg53022.htm), original witness testimony and congressional questions. All three 2023 profiles link to **this one underlying hearing**, not three independent government instrument tests.
2. **2024 US House hearing** — [official GovInfo original transcript (13 November 2024)](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-118hhrg57440/html/CHRG-118hhrg57440.htm), including Gallaudet's first-person email account, Elizondo and Shellenberger's claims, Gold's policy recommendation. These four profiles share **one hearing document**, not four independent agency evidentiary validations.
3. **2004 encounter, separate named witness:** [CBS 60 Minutes original 16 May 2021 interview with Fravor and Dietrich](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/ufo-military-intelligence-60-minutes-2021-05-16/). Dietrich was a different pilot present at the **same incident**; useful firsthand corroboration of a shared observation, but not a second sighting or independently certified original radar geometry.
4. **Go Fast technical counter-analysis:** [AARO official 6 February 2025 case-resolution and methodology report](https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/case_resolution_reports/AARO_GoFast_Case_Resolution_Card_Methodology_Final.pdf). It assessed an approximate altitude of 13,000 feet and wind-adjusted speed 5–92 mph, without anomalous acceleration in the public clip; it explicitly says original source file/metadata and crew interviews were unavailable. **This is relevant to the filmed object's apparent speed—not proof Gallaudet's email was or was not deleted.**
5. **Broader historical challenge:** [AARO's March 2024 historical report](https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/AARO_Historical_Record_Report_Volume_1_2024.pdf) sets out official conclusions about examined covert retrieval/reverse-engineering allegations, with stated scope limits and a date **before** the November 2024 hearing. Not a fully declassified, exhaustive rebuttal to every later source or programme name.

## Seven individual original-record comparisons

### David Grusch — `grusch-2023-house-claims-briefing`

**Precisely scoped statement:** David Grusch told Congress in July 2023 that he had received accounts from others alleging concealed UAP crash-retrieval and reverse-engineering activity.

**Direct hearing location:** [26 July 2023 transcript, Statement of David Grusch; Rep. Burlison questions about seeing alleged spacecraft or bodies](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-118hhrg53022/html/CHRG-118hhrg53022.htm)

**What the original record establishes:** Official sworn transcript confirms Grusch reported interviews with people claiming direct knowledge of retrievals. He explicitly told Representative Burlison that he had not personally seen alleged bodies; he declined to describe what he had seen regarding craft in open session. Separate testimony that he viewed unexplained imagery is NOT a publicly presented recovered-craft sample.

**Independence and duplicate-source handling:** Grusch interviewing a purported direct witness is secondhand for the physical recovery assertion. Congressional repetition of the same interview story and later reporter summaries are not independent physical corroboration; exact witnesses and material have not been disclosed for public inspection.

**Alternative or conflicting public record:** AARO's March 2024 historical review reached contrary findings for the programmes it examined. The 2024 committee chair also reported difficulty substantiating Grusch's prior allegations from responding agencies. Institutional findings are not a fully declassified check of each confidential allegation.

**What is still required:** Independently identifiable original insider evidence, unredacted chain of custody, publicly inspectable hardware/biological material, contemporaneous programme files and a named second-editor review of witness independence remain unavailable for this audit.

### Ryan Graves — `graves-2023-house-aviation-safety`

**Precisely scoped statement:** Ryan Graves publicly testified in July 2023 about aviation safety concerns and reports of unidentified objects from aircrew.

**Direct hearing location:** [26 July 2023 transcript, opening Statement of Ryan Graves: 2014 Oceana radar upgrade, W-72 near-miss narrative and pilot safety reports](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-118hhrg53022/html/CHRG-118hhrg53022.htm)

**What the original record establishes:** Graves testified to his own 2014 radar and sensor observations, and relayed a particularly close near-miss reported by OTHER Navy flight crew. The W-72 50-foot cube-inside-sphere near-miss must not be described as an event personally witnessed inside his own cockpit.

**Independence and duplicate-source handling:** The hearing is a first-party source for Graves's testimony, not release of the original radar tracks, mission commander debrief or specific safety report. Repeated flight-safety reporting is relevant to risk management but does not independently establish an unknown object's anomalous physical origin.

**Alternative or conflicting public record:** NASA's general 2023 UAP study cautions about inadequate calibrated data and has no case-specific adjudication of the Graves W-72 near-miss. FAA reporting procedures demonstrate an administrative route, not incident corroboration.

**What is still required:** Obtain contemporaneous UAP/flight-safety reports, pilot identities and independent original first-person description of the near miss, sensor telemetry and radar-system calibration data to test distance/speed/shape.

### David Fravor — `fravor-2023-house-2004-encounter`

**Precisely scoped statement:** David Fravor told Congress in July 2023 about a firsthand 2004 military aviation encounter with an unidentified object.

**Direct hearing location:** [26 July 2023 transcript, Statement of David Fravor and questions about the 14 November 2004 Nimitz Tic Tac](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-118hhrg53022/html/CHRG-118hhrg53022.htm)

**What the original record establishes:** Fravor testified he personally saw an unidentified object in November 2004. Another named pilot from the same flight, Alex Dietrich, publicly described her own observation in an original CBS 60 Minutes interview; their testimony concerns the SAME event.

**Independence and duplicate-source handling:** Dietrich provides a separate named firsthand eyewitness perspective on the shared encounter, not an independent second incident or validation of precise extraordinary velocity/acceleration. The later infrared video released by DoD was made on a subsequent sortie and must not be misrepresented as continuous footage of Fravor's initial encounter.

**Alternative or conflicting public record:** The 2020 DoD announcement authenticated three historic Navy clips and said depicted phenomena were unidentified. It did not publicly certify the object origin, all velocities, earlier ship radar data or the complete claimed encounter sequence. CBS is a publisher of interviews, not an independent radar laboratory.

**What is still required:** A contemporaneous complete USS Princeton tracking dataset, intact original FLIR metadata, flight logs, unedited eyewitness accounts and independently calculated positional uncertainties are not public in this review.

### Mike Gold — `gold-2024-nasa-uap-research-proposals`

**Precisely scoped statement:** Mike Gold recommended in November 2024 testimony that NASA encourage open scientific study, improve UAP data collection and consider dedicated analysis methods and instruments.

**Direct hearing location:** [13 November 2024 transcript, Statement of Michael Gold, explicit declaration that he spoke personally and recommended UAP science without stigma](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-118hhrg57440/html/CHRG-118hhrg57440.htm)

**What the original record establishes:** Gold publicly argued for more rigorous evidence gathering, scientific study and data-sharing. He specifically stated he spoke for himself, not for NASA or his then employer; the hearing does not convert his recommendations into a claim to have witnessed recovered non-human hardware.

**Independence and duplicate-source handling:** The NASA 2023 UAP independent-study report discusses scientific data limitations and methods, aligning with a general policy direction; an institutional methodology and Gold's separate proposal should not be scored as two verified physical UAP incidents.

**Alternative or conflicting public record:** Alternative scientific approaches and implementation costs can be debated, but his role and advocacy are not an adjudicable anomalous-object encounter. No purported unknown-origin physical data should be inferred from this policy testimony.

**What is still required:** To evaluate feasibility, obtain specifications, data access conditions, validation studies, instrument budgets and proposed oversight; a personal/UAP-event percentage remains Not applicable regardless of how good the recommendation is.

### Tim Gallaudet — `gallaudet-2024-navy-safety-email`

**Precisely scoped statement:** Tim Gallaudet testified in November 2024 that he received a January 2015 Navy safety-of-flight email with the Go Fast video attached, and that the email later disappeared from his account.

**Direct hearing location:** [13 November 2024 transcript, Statement of Tim Gallaudet, opening account of 2015 URGENT SAFETY OF FLIGHT ISSUE email and claimed following-day deletion](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-118hhrg57440/html/CHRG-118hhrg57440.htm)

**What the original record establishes:** Gallaudet's own sworn statement confirms he publicly asserted receiving the 2015 email with Go Fast footage and that it later disappeared from recipients' accounts. The official transcript is evidence of the claim being made, not direct forensic proof that a particular message was remotely deleted.

**Independence and duplicate-source handling:** The Navy's release of Go Fast footage confirms a genuine military-recorded video, not that the asserted safety email was sent or removed. No original message headers, mail-server retention/audit log or independently verifiable recipient account record is supplied publicly in these sources.

**Alternative or conflicting public record:** AARO's 6 February 2025 Go Fast technical report assessed approximately 13,000-foot object altitude and wind-adjusted approximate 5–92 mph speed with no anomalous speed. That challenges speed/origin interpretation, NOT the alleged email receipt, deletion or classification mechanism. AARO noted original source file/metadata were unavailable and lacked aircrew interviews.

**What is still required:** Original Fleet Forces message, exact time/recipient routing, system access and retention audit logs, named witness receipts, complete FLIR original file and independent Navy mailbox review remain necessary to verify the deletion proposition.

## Corrections and overclaim safeguards

- **Grusch's public admissions:** The congressional questions distinguish interviewing purported direct witnesses and having personally examined certain unexplained imagery from witnessing an alleged recovered biological specimen. Do not turn a closed-session offer into an accessible classified evidence record.
- **Graves and the 2014 W-72 near miss:** He publicly described his own squadron's sensor encounters and relayed the close approach reported by a *different* flight crew. Do not attribute the 50-foot near miss as a cockpit experience Graves directly saw.
- **Fravor and Dietrich:** Separate named eyewitnesses provide genuine but event-correlated direct accounts; a subsequent sortie's infrared recording is not the original flight's unedited continuous footage.
- **Gallaudet and Go Fast:** The email-reporting account, its disappearance and the object-speed inference are **different, separately testable propositions**. The later AARO analysis deals with the video-speed interpretation using a 34-second publicly available recording; it cannot independently prove or disprove mail-server tampering.
- **Shellenberger:** A House-submitted anonymous-source document is an original **allegation document**, not an authenticated programme budget, signed authorisation or classified sensor download. March 2024 AARO reporting predated the later publicly named programme.
- **Gold:** He expressly testified in a **personal** capacity, not for NASA. His recommendation to collect scientific data is a policy/methodology proposal. Its site and community incident scores remain **Not applicable**, not 0/100.
- **Elizondo:** Original testimony confirms that an assertion was made; biographical roles or a sworn hearing do not substitute for release of source hardware, engineering records or independent sample testing.
