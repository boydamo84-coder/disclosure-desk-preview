## Jeremy Corbell — original CNN interview
Original broadcaster text: https://transcripts.cnn.com/show/ebo/date/2021-05-31/segment/01

Checked publisher **rush transcript** of *Erin Burnett OutFront*, 31 May 2021, around **19:50:35**, lines 206–229. CNN quotes a DoD spokesperson acknowledging that **referenced photos and video were recorded by Navy personnel** and included in UAP Task Force examination. Corbell's own remarks include claims that the USS Omaha ship was surrounded by **more than fourteen** UAP and that released footage suggested advanced capabilities or apparent entry into the sea. The transcript also carries Poppy Harlow's question relaying retired astronaut Scott Kelly's possible optical illusion/drone alternative.

**Finding:** Attribution and narrow Navy-recording provenance are documented, while exact radar counts, tracking geometry, identification and extraordinary performance are **not** proven by the quoted DoD response or by CNN's edited clip. CNN labels its document a **rush transcript subject to correction**. Original raw ship/radar data, full filmed media provenance, timings and independent reconstruction were not inspected.

## George Knapp — original seven-page 2025 written congressional submission
Official original: https://oversight.house.gov/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/George-Knapp-Written-Testimony.pdf

Directly read the accessible PDF text and **visually checked original PDF pages 1, 3, 5 and 7** (zero-index 0/2/4/6) with separate source screenshots. Four passages:
- **Page 1:** Knapp introduces himself as an investigative journalist, explicitly **not** a first-person UFO witness or whistleblower.
- **Page 2:** says more than two dozen informants discussed strands of the Lazar/S-4 story, including claims of intimidation. Private newsroom source reports and personal investigation are not independently authenticated physical recoveries.
- **Page 5:** recounts alleged Lockheed–Bigelow/BAASS unusual-material transfer negotiations but acknowledges **the transfer agreement was not completed**. No original material test or fully published transaction evidence is provided.
- **Page 7:** relates Russian witness/source accounts and says materials were submitted to the task force; he closes with a signed journalist identification. Publicly reading the submission does not access every submitted Russian original, independent translation or classified investigative chain.

The PDF's authentic House publication supports the **fact of written testimony** and accurately credits the original speaker, **not** an externally inspected recovered craft. Repeated interviews and Knapp's other reporting are frequently the same source network; do not tally them as independent specimens.

## Steven Greer — 2001 conference, original reporting versus event record
Contemporaneous report: https://abcnews.go.com/Technology/story?id=98572&page=1  
Library archival catalogue of edited 2001 VHS: https://search.worldcat.org/title/52741633

ABC News original article **9–10 May 2001**, lines 8–15, independently records a National Press Club event with **about twenty** participating former military/government figures and quotes Greer alleging the testimony established extraterrestrial presence. Greer also asserted a larger pool of **around 400 people willing to testify**: that number is **Greer's claim**, not 400 independently published depositions or personally witnessed objects. WorldCat original 2001 publication entry identifies an **edited video** of the event. That is evidence the event recording was published, not that this review watched all the film or checked each participant's technical source records. No full unedited conference tape or 400 primary depositions were inspected in M28e. Independent press coverage and library cataloguing establish the **public event and statement**, not its extraordinary interpretation.

## Jacques Vallée and Garry Nolan — ONE shared peer-reviewed laboratory paper

Publisher original (direct full text blocked **HTTP 403**): https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0376042121000907  
DOI: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.paerosci.2021.100788

This is **one** publication: Nolan, Vallée, Jiang and Lemke, *Progress in Aerospace Sciences* volume 128, article 100788 (**2022**), based on the **same parent specimen CB_JV-1**. The original publisher was inaccessible directly during M28e; publicly indexed publisher abstract and section/conclusion snippets were checked rather than silently claiming a full original-paper review. Third-party full-text mirrors exist but are **not** independent original publisher or author sources and were not used to declare a complete methods review.

The indexed original abstract describes elemental/spatial differences across **five** Council Bluffs parent-material subsample grains, while noting **no statistically significant isotopic deviation from terrestrial normal** and acknowledging the material may be terrestrial-derived. Original collection provenance, function and alleged connection to any unusual aerial object remain unresolved. Neither authorship nor the result proves manufacture by a non-human technology. The Vallée and Nolan profiles have **different biographical subjects but one source-data lineage** (shared group `council-bluffs-1977-one-paper-100788`). It must not double-count evidence in any later criterion-by-criterion analysis. Missing raw calibrated spectra, original sample-handling chain, external blind controls, independent remeasurement and technical reviewer.

## James Fox — original filmmaker pages and Brazilian archival date conflict
Original programme descriptions:
- https://filmschoolradio.com/moment-of-contact-director-james-fox/
- https://filmschoolradio.com/the-program-director-james-fox/

The original **2022 Moment of Contact** filmmaker interview page explicitly concerns alleged 1996 events in **Varginha, Brazil**, and explains the documentary's reported witness/interview premise. The later **2024 The Program** director biography refers to **1966**, contradicting its own earlier website account and the official Brazilian military inquiry's date. Treat **1966** as an apparent **publisher biography error**, not a second established incident or an accurate replacement for 1996.

Brazil's **Superior Tribunal Militar ARQUIMEDES** indexed original institutional catalogue describes **IPM 18/1997** (February–June 1997), examining allegations of supposed military handling of a creature said to date from **20 January 1996**. Official archive catalogue search was accessible via a search-index result, but direct opening of selected archival table URLs was **rejected**; M28e did **not** read every inquiry volume or independently translate its original conclusions. An administrative inquiry index supports the existence and date of an investigation, **not proof** the alleged incident or beings were real.

No full documentary footage or unedited original witness testimony was played, matched to Portuguese originals or independently validated.

Original author URL: https://nickpope.net/wpte19/rendlesham-forest/

The author-published *Rendlesham Forest* page remains **publicly indexed** with its relevant passages, but a fresh direct fetch to nickpope.net redirects to a host inaccessible to the research tool. We therefore record **indexed author text and incomplete direct page access**, not a fully inspected original 2013 webpage or a dated snapshot.

In the indexed article Pope **explicitly says the 1980 events occurred before he joined the MoD**, identifies his UFO desk work as **1991–1994**, and describes a later **cold-case review**. He argues that proposed conventional explanations did not fit the facts as he understood them, while also quoting MoD's public **no defence significance** response and admitting he does not know the US inquiry results.

This verifies the **content of his published interpretation**, not that Pope was an original eyewitness, nor original radar returns, a landing, unambiguous technological origin or a final MoD official endorsement of his interpretation. The 1980 original Halt/duty/document sources and dated 2013 presentation remain separately to be checked.
