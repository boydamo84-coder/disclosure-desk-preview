# Who's Who Milestone 12 — original technical claims and independent competing-source ledger

**Research date:** 15 November 2026 · **Branch:** `feature/who-evidence-score-framework-20261115` · **Status:** unsigned primary-analysis draft, **no site score and no community ballot**.

## Methodology and what this is not

This source review distinguishes **measurement**, **model inference** and **interpretation**. Academic publishing verifies that analysis was performed and publicly described; it does **not** by itself authenticate that a recovered object came from a particular meteor, that a video captured an extraordinary flight manoeuvre, or that numerical acceleration was measured by a released original radar track. Informal forum disagreement is not peer-reviewed laboratory falsification. A second named independent editor, reproducible data provenance and competing evidence are still required for any 0–100 editorial criterion points. The 0–100 scale is a proposed **documented-evidence-strength rubric**, not a statement about a person's honesty or statistical probability that aliens exist.

**This first pass covers five individual sourceAudit records attached to four distinct technical research subjects**: Mick West's Gimbal model; Avi Loeb's IM1 spherule sampling; Nolan/Vallée's *single shared* CB_JV-1 paper (listed under both authors but **one** publication and sample); Kevin Knuth/Powell/Reali conditional flight calculations. The model's other coauthors are original investigators, not independent corroborators.

## Source-verification highlights

**Gimbal:** [West's 13 March 2022 original post and public simulator/code](https://www.metabunk.org/threads/gimbal-ufo-a-new-analysis.12333/) explicitly presents *possible* IR glare caused by pod/camera motion; it leaves the precise filmed object unidentified. A separate [2026 technical debate](https://www.metabunk.org/threads/the-gimbal-ufo-marik-claims-new-findings-falsify-prosaic-explanations.14839/) addresses early-frame rotation and cloud-pan geometry, with [further discussions/West replies](https://www.metabunk.org/threads/the-gimbal-ufo-marik-claims-new-findings-falsify-prosaic-explanations.14839/page-2). The debate uses the public video and competing analyses; neither group publishes the original uncompressed ATFLIR calibration data in these cited threads. The DoD [27 April 2020 Navy footage release](https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/2165713/statement-by-the-department-of-defense-on-the-release-of-historical-navy-videos/) confirms a military video exists, not that West's proposed cause or any extraordinary origin has been proven.

**IM1/Loeb:** The [author-hosted original 2024 *Chemical Geology* 670, article 122415 PDF](https://galileo.hsites.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum6371/files/chemical_classification_of_spherules_1-s2.0-s0009254124004959-main.pdf) displays a received date in February, online publication **18 September 2024**, and issue print date **20 December 2024**; do not treat these as two papers. Its first-page abstract describes approximately 850 collected spherules and an unusual Be-La-U-enriched subset, but section 3 explicitly states the team cannot *prove* that any sampled material belongs to the predicted IM1 bolide. The [independently authored 2025 *Chemical Geology* article 122628](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S000925412500018X) argues the disputed D-type spherules are volcanic; this is a peer-reviewed competing interpretation, not a settled reanalysis of every collected sample. A [different 2025 *Chemical Geology* article 122627 coauthored by Loeb](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0009254125000178) classifies some shapes as likely terrestrial and suggests a mixed possibility for others; this same-expedition team's follow-up is **not** a disinterested replication. Even an unambiguous meteoritic origin would not demonstrate spacecraft construction.

**Council Bluffs/Nolan/Vallée:** The [single Nolan/Vallée/Jiang/Lemke original *Progress in Aerospace Sciences* 128 (2022) 100788 publication](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0376042121000907) reports elemental inhomogeneity across five sampled grains, but the isotope measurements showed no significant difference from normal terrestrial composition in examined subsamples. The publisher abstract explicitly permits terrestrial-derived materials and leaves the parent sample's provenance and purpose unknown. The [persistent DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.paerosci.2021.100788) points to **the same paper**, not independent replication. A [public methodological critique discussion](https://www.metabunk.org/threads/is-improved-instrumental-techniques-nolan-vallee-jiang-lemke-2022-a-useful-paper.13286/) questions photos/specimen identification and isotopic interpretation; label it as informal discussion, NOT a peer-reviewed contrary mass-spectrometry replication. Neither the historical 1977 witnesses nor two modern coauthors independently prove exotic metal manufacturing; exact custody and controls matter.

**Knuth model:** [Knuth, Powell and Reali, *Entropy* 21(10) 939 (25 September 2019)](https://www.mdpi.com/1099-4300/21/10/939) reports an estimated **modal 5,370 g** for the particular model of a claimed ~8,530-metre radar-observed descent lasting **0.78 ± 0.08 seconds**, under a chosen accelerate/decelerate trajectory. The source of that descent in the paper is Kevin Day's reported observations/personal communication, **not** a published 2004 radar file containing directly measured instantaneous accelerations. The separate [University at Albany publication index](https://scholarsarchive.library.albany.edu/physics_fac_scholar/58/) is the same paper, not independent replication. The paper itself lists observation error/fabrication as alternatives to advanced technology; the arithmetic remains conditional. Distinguish **0.78 s** in the paper from media transcription sometimes reporting 78 seconds. The exact raw geometry and times are needed before scoring real-world flight performance.

## Five named claim records and their editorial boundaries

### Mick West — `west-gimbal-2022-glare-interpretation`

**Attributed technical claim:** In a 2022 analysis, Mick West proposed that the apparent rotation in the publicly released Navy Gimbal video could be caused by sensor glare and camera motion rather than an object visibly rotating.

**Original primary source:** [Read the exact original](https://www.metabunk.org/threads/gimbal-ufo-a-new-analysis.12333/) — 13 March 2022 opening post by Mick West; four claimed observables; Gimbal simulator and downloadable source-code/data links

**What the original record actually establishes:** West's own technical model argues the apparent rotation of the Gimbal infrared image can arise from a camera/pod rotation and hot-target glare, not necessarily physical rotation of the unknown target. He supplies a public simulation and working source data. This is an attributable optical interpretation, not an instrument-origin determination or a demonstration that a particular jet was filmed.

**Independent corroboration versus re-used source or author:** The simulator, the original forum post and West's accompanying video are outputs of the same analysis, NOT three independent sensor validations. Independent repeat implementation should reproduce the predicted angular changes under stated pod settings; released US DoD footage is genuine Navy recording, but not calibrated internal sensor telemetry. Pilot remarks about other radar contacts need their own original time-correlated tracks.

**Contradictory or alternative source record:** In the 2026 technical debate, critics challenge the model's account of early-frame rotation and cloud-camera pan, while West disputes their interpretation; these are competing analyses of the same public video and not peer-reviewed conclusive raw-calibration findings. Neither a glare hypothesis nor unexplained footage alone establishes the object's precise identity or extraterrestrial origin.

**Evidence still missing:** Original uncompressed Gimbal sensor recording, ATFLIR calibration and detailed gimbal-angle logs, range/aspect metadata, environmental cloud motion measurements, independent reproduction and second expert review are still needed to adjudicate the optical rotation and any target identity.

### Avi Loeb — `loeb-2024-im1-spherule-origin-interpretation`

**Attributed technical claim:** Avi Loeb and colleagues published a 2024 geochemical classification of Pacific seafloor spherules recovered near the predicted IM1 trajectory, interpreting several compositions as cosmic or potentially unusual.

**Original primary source:** [Read the exact original](https://galileo.hsites.harvard.edu/sites/g/files/omnuum6371/files/chemical_classification_of_spherules_1-s2.0-s0009254124004959-main.pdf) — Loeb et al. original Chemical Geology 670 (2024) 122415 PDF, abstract page 1, section 3 'Spherule samples and location' page 3; published online 18 September 2024

**What the original record actually establishes:** The authors describe recovering approximately 850 magnetically selected seafloor spherules near a predicted fireball path, measure chemistry and identify a D-type / BeLaU-enriched subset. Section 3 explicitly says that no recovered material can be definitively attributed to IM1. Real lab measurements and a possible unusual chemical provenance do not prove the particles originate from that specific bolide, are interstellar, or represent manufactured technology.

**Independent corroboration versus re-used source or author:** The same Galileo expedition's press releases and the Loeb-author 2025 follow-up are NOT independent laboratories or a new unrelated recovery. The 2025 coauthored morphology study itself finds blocky and vesicular D-type particles probably terrestrial, with other textured subsets under consideration; these are interpretations of related collected material, not independent confirmation of extrasolar manufacture.

**Contradictory or alternative source record:** An independently authored 2025 Chemical Geology re-evaluation (122628) argues that most claimed cosmic particles, especially the debated D-types, are terrestrial volcanic rather than meteoritic. The Loeb-coauthored 2025 companion article (122627) offers a more mixed morphological classification; competing publications disagree on origin and should be compared with sample-level data, not tallied as separate extraterrestrial confirmations.

**Evidence still missing:** Publicly validated IM1 strewn-field trajectory uncertainties, contamination and industrial/volcanic background sampling, blinded cross-laboratory sample analyses and original chain of custody linking any given spherule directly to IM1 are not established in the cited work. No independently established physical artefact or non-human engineering demonstrated.

### Garry Nolan — `nolan-2022-council-bluffs-material-analysis`

**Attributed technical claim:** In a jointly authored 2022 journal article, Garry Nolan and colleagues reported elemental variation but no significant deviations from terrestrial-normal isotopic ratios in examined subsamples of Council Bluffs sample CB_JV-1.

**Original primary source:** [Read the exact original](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0376042121000907) — Progress in Aerospace Sciences 128 (1 January 2022), article 100788, abstract and Council Bluffs CB_JV-1 case study; five subsample grains and isotope comparison

**What the original record actually establishes:** The shared Nolan–Vallée–Jiang–Lemke paper reports spatial/elemental heterogeneity among five CB_JV-1 subsample grains but no statistically significant deviation from terrestrial-normal isotope ratios for tested grains. The original authors explicitly allow terrestrial-derived constituents and leave the sample's provenance and function unknown; isotope/element differences are not evidence of confirmed exotic manufacture.

**Independent corroboration versus re-used source or author:** Jacques Vallée and Garry Nolan coauthored the same publication and worked from the same Council Bluffs parent specimen. Its DOI and publisher version are two access routes to ONE paper. Historical witness recounting and method discussions cannot be counted as separate blinded modern laboratory replicates.

**Contradictory or alternative source record:** A public critical methodology thread queries the photographed specimen identification, representation of grains and missing complete chain of custody. That thread is an informal critique and not an independently performed laboratory analysis. The paper itself reports terrestrial-compatible isotopes, which is the most important scientific limit; NASA-wide measurement advice is not direct sample refutation.

**Evidence still missing:** Complete 1977-to-present specimen transfers/handling, authentic raw NanoSIMS and MIBI error/calibration details, blinded control specimens, source-identification tests and a truly independent outside-laboratory replication are still needed before conclusions about origin or engineering.

### Kevin Knuth — `knuth-2019-nimitz-acceleration-estimates`

**Attributed technical claim:** Kevin Knuth and coauthors published 2019 estimates of extreme minimum accelerations for reported UAP manoeuvres, including Nimitz encounter accounts, conditional on reported distances and time intervals.

**Original primary source:** [Read the exact original](https://www.mdpi.com/1099-4300/21/10/939) — Entropy 21(10):939, published 25 September 2019; 'Nimitz encounters' radar report model; Figure 3 acceleration posterior and input assumptions

**What the original record actually establishes:** Knuth, Powell and Reali calculate a conditional estimate of around 5,370 g (mode) for a supposed 8,530-metre altitude change in 0.78 seconds using reported radar observations, 0.78±0.08-s timing uncertainty and an accelerate-half / decelerate-half model. This is a real published calculation UNDER THE ASSUMED motion inputs, not a public instrument-measured 5,370-g flight event.

**Independent corroboration versus re-used source or author:** The University at Albany faculty archive lists the same Entropy paper and DOI, not an independent radar measurement or replication. The descent input references Senior Chief Kevin Day's personal communication/recounted radar observations; published 2004 original sensor data for precisely the modelled descent were not produced in this paper. Fravor's separate 2023 sworn eyewitness encounter does not provide measured radar timestamps for Day's claimed descent.

**Contradictory or alternative source record:** The 2019 paper explicitly recognises alternative possibilities of observation fabrication or serious error versus unusual technology, while also favouring the technology interpretation. The NASA 2023 UAP scientific report emphasises instrument calibration and distance context but does not independently reconstruct this specific reported descent. A mathematical calculation's validity cannot establish that its input observations occurred as described.

**Evidence still missing:** Declassified 2004 original radar tracks and time stamps, independent source validation of the estimated 0.78-second drop, reconciled ship/platform geometry, error and sensitivity analysis under alternative motion models, and second-editor physical-data check remain missing. A 78-second retelling, if encountered in media, must not silently replace the paper's 0.78-second input.

## Source-group anti-inflation safeguards

- `council-bluffs-1977-one-paper-100788` is attached to **both Nolan and Vallée**, but denotes **one paper, one sample study, one DOI**. It must not be totalled twice in any evidence rubric or counts of replicating laboratories.
- `im1-2023-single-expedition` links Loeb's 2024 collection and later author-involved re-analysis. The 2025 independent critique is a separate team and publication. A new article by an overlapping sample team is **not** automatically an independent collection.
- `nimitz-2004-day-secondhand-model` captures the provenance of Knuth's reported high-acceleration inputs. University repository and Entropy are the **same** original research; 2023 Fravor eyewitness testimony concerns a reported shared encounter, not the model's raw radar track.
- `gimbal-2015-single-video` concerns the **same released Gimbal clip** interpreted with competing image models. Video authenticity is not independently repeated imaging, a known identity or proof of either explanatory hypothesis.
