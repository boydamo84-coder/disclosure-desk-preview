## Adversarial source-reuse rules
- **Literal URL reuse:** same public URL, even with a tracking parameter, trailing slash or section fragment, is not additional independent corroboration.
- **Same source, different surface:** DOI resolver, publisher landing, mirrored PDF, event calendar and article quoting the same testimony may refer to one underlying publication. Their *publication identity* needs human checking; automatic canonicalisation is a cue, not a verdict.
- **Same event, different testimony:** distinct witness statements at one hearing can be separately attributed firsthand accounts while sharing the same record. Never merge their words; never equate hearing co-attendance with separate sensor corroboration.
- **Same incident, different claim:** two Rendlesham observers may describe different nights and events. A shared case family is a review warning, not proof that either account is identical or false.
- **Temporal mismatch:** a March 2024 broad AARO statement cannot independently adjudicate a specifically alleged January 2025 Barber retrieval merely by being cited in its bibliography.
- **Methodological context versus case evidence:** general NASA FAQs and broad AARO statements can show methodological alternatives and institutional policy, but cannot automatically become case-specific measurements.
- **Role/proposal records:** Mellon and Gold are *not applicable* to an incident-origin evidence score; attribution of public policy statements is not an incident verdict.
- **Source gaps:** a dead page, blocked journal access, private video or indexed snippet is not a full original investigation; flag `access_limited` rather than filling gaps with assumptions.

## Confidential independent-review process (proposed, NOT implemented in the public site)
1. Assign a qualified **lead human reviewer** and a separately qualified **second human reviewer** outside the public GitHub repository. Record dated source version, conflict-of-interest declaration and reviewer scope; do not store names or private-source attachments in public JSON.
2. For each specific claim, document original-record access, source lineage, distinct direct/raw records, case-specific contrary evidence, reproducibility/uncertainty, and rights/privacy (the six M29 checks). Quote short scoped excerpts with exact publication locator.
3. Maintain a **source-change ledger**: `source URL`, `publisher`, `publication date`, `last checked UTC`, `access status`, `exact original version or archive identifier`, `record relationship`, `license or permission state`, and `editor note`. This document proposes the fields but does not pretend a live monitoring system exists.
4. **Recheck cadence proposal:** mutable news/creator pages every 30 days while actively reviewed, public event indexes every 90 days where later uploads are possible, and persistent GPO/published DOI records every 180 days for corrections/addenda. An elapsed interval only raises an alert; it never changes the truth or independently proves a claim. No recurring job is installed here.
5. **Fail closed:** until independent review and methodology approval, preserve `score unavailable / human review pending`, never assign points to a person, and never enable voting. A later numeric rubric is **claim-specific** only and needs transparent uncertainty and clearly inspectable evidence.
