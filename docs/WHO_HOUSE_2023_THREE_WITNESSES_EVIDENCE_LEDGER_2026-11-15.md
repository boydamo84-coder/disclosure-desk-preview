This source ledger advances the `Who’s Who` evidence-review milestone for **David Grusch**, **Ryan Graves** and **David Fravor**. ## David Grusch — retrieval / reverse engineering allegations

**Precisely attributed draft claim:** In July 2023, Grusch publicly alleged that people he interviewed and information he received concerned a concealed UAP retrieval/reverse engineering programme.

**Primary record:** GPO transcript, `STATEMENT OF DAVID GRUSCH`, especially the discussion of information received from others and the alleged programme. The transcript explicitly distinguishes that he says he was **informed** of a programme; it is not a publicly inspectable programme log, material sample, or validated record of recovered non-human technology.

**Relevant institutional contrary assessment:** Department of Defense March 8 2024 statement on AARO's historical review: https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/3700894/statement-by-pentagon-press-secretary-maj-gen-pat-ryder-on-the-historical-recor/ . The department reported that AARO had not found verifiable evidence of extraterrestrial reverse-engineering projects and gave alternative interpretations of reported programmes. This assessment is **broader than this single witness**; do not claim it individually disproves everything Grusch privately described. Equally, do not claim hearing testimony validates any programme.

**Open evidence questions:** Which specific underlying records can be made publicly inspectable? Are purported sources independent of one another? Can a programme name, date, facility or object be verified in a suitable non-classified primary record? What alternative programme interpretations and competing assessments apply to each precisely specified allegation? **No scoring until these checks are documented.**

## Ryan Graves — aircrew reports / aviation safety

**Precisely attributed draft claim:** Graves testified concerning his own operational observations, other pilots' reported experiences and possible aviation safety risks.

**Primary record:** GPO transcript, `STATEMENT OF RYAN GRAVES`, especially the 2014 radar observations, sensor correlation, claimed close passage of aircraft and proposed reporting mechanism. The testimony clearly contains both personal experience and information about other aircrew; the public transcript is not a complete independently verified radar dataset or flight-safety incident file.

**Current independent policy context:** FAA Air Traffic Order JO 7210.3 section **4-7-4, Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena Reports**: https://www.faa.gov/air_traffic/publications/atpubs/foa_html/chap4_section_7.html . The current FAA procedure requires appropriate reporting of pilot/ATC UAP observations; that supports the present reporting-policy context, **not independent verification of a 2014 event**.

**Data-quality context:** NASA UAP FAQs, including limits from incomplete measurements: https://science.nasa.gov/uap/faqs/ . NASA's general point on incomplete observational data does not determine the true explanation of Graves's reported cases.

**Open evidence questions:** Can the reported safety event be linked to an inspectable contemporary report, flight log, sensor record or separate named participant? Are the other pilot accounts independent? Which inferences concern the hazard of **an unidentified object** versus claims about its performance or origin? **No scoring until separately assessed.**

## David Fravor — 2004 Nimitz encounter

**Precisely attributed draft claim:** Fravor gave firsthand testimony about a reported 2004 unidentified object encounter during naval operations.

**Primary record:** GPO transcript, `STATEMENT OF DAVID FRAVOR (RET.)`, including his own observations, his account of radar-controller information, and his statement that a **later crew** acquired footage. The recording is a different public evidence item from an uninterrupted record of Fravor's own cockpit view.

**Separate witness account:** CBS *60 Minutes* firsthand interview with Alex Dietrich and David Fravor: https://www.cbsnews.com/news/ufo-military-intelligence-60-minutes-2021-08-29/ . Dietrich independently describes an unidentified object on the same flight. This supports that at least two participants described an encounter; it does **not** by itself validate the object’s inferred speed, propulsion method or origin. This is an interview hosted by a publisher, not a raw military instrument download.

**Authenticated video provenance, limited meaning:** DoD statement, **27 April 2020**, describing three released Navy videos (one from **November 2004**): https://www.defense.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/2165713/statement-by-the-department-of-defense-on-the-release-of-historical-navy-videos/ . The department described the phenomena as unidentified; it did **not** affirm extraordinary physical performance, external origin or that Fravor's aircraft shot the published footage.

**General scientific caution:** NASA UAP FAQs: https://science.nasa.gov/uap/faqs/ ; a lack of complete calibrated observations restricts confident conclusions about what is depicted. This is context, not a particular resolution of the Nimitz event.

**Open evidence questions:** Locate and validate any genuinely original radar tracks and their chain of custody; confirm exact linkage between released footage and each sighting; evaluate range, calibration, environmental and operator details; identify which eyewitness observations are mutually independent; compare credible alternative analyses. **No numerical score is cleared.**
