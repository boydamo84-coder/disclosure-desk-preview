# Who's Who milestone 10 — 27 individually documented role/work evidence-scope decisions

**Research indexed 15 November 2026 | Development branch only | Not a completed expert fact-check or a public rating**

## Why this stage matters

All 50 existing Who's Who people now have one of two **distinct** individually documented research dispositions. **23** have a narrow claim-specific evidence draft with original sources and relevant alternatives; none is currently approved for numerical scoring. The remaining **27** have an individually explained, source-linked determination that their documented study role, congressional record or government-records work **is not itself an extraordinary UAP incident claim**. Their status is **Not applicable to this documented role/work**, **NOT 0/100 for that person's honesty, beliefs, or any other UAP claim**. If a separate, dated and precisely attributable physical-event statement is found, it must get an independent claim card.

The 27 decisions use only public original role/source records: 16 NASA roles (the other NASA panel member Mike Gold already has a separate unscored scientific-policy proposal), seven 2025 House task-force appointees, two 2023 Senate UAP records-act amendment sponsors, one other House oversight participant, and one FOIA records specialist. These are a source-scope **research draft**, not a signed independent verification of every biographical detail or a finding that a person has never made any other allegation. All 60 scope reference entries below reuse or supplement existing public source references and **are not 60 independent physical-event confirmations**.

## Original institutional comparison

- **NASA 21 October 2022 selection:** https://www.nasa.gov/general/nasa-announces-unidentified-aerial-phenomena-study-team-members/ — 16 chosen panel members, chair David Spergel and distinct study-organising official Daniel Evans. Team scope was how to use unclassified information and scientific tools, **not** forensic verification of recovered hardware.
- **NASA 14 September 2023 independent study report:** https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/uap-independent-study-team-final-report.pdf — named report roster and methodological conclusions. Collective scientific standards must not be falsely attributed to one individual's case observation.
- **House 18 February 2025 appointees:** https://oversight.house.gov/release/chairman-comer-and-rep-luna-announce-republican-appointments-to-declassification-and-transparency-task-force/ — Luna, Mace, Burchett, Boebert, Burlison, Crane and Gill. Appointments indicate oversight authority, not witness corroboration.
- **House hearing 9 September 2025:** https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CHRG-119hhrg61718/html/CHRG-119hhrg61718.htm — formal hearing, chair's opening and distinct participant list, including other attendees such as Jared Moskowitz. Luna's separate Eglin allegation is **flagged for future precise event-level extraction**, not given a percentage based on an opening statement.
- **Senate amendment SA 797, 13 July 2023:** https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/CREC-2023-07-13/html/CREC-2023-07-13-pt1-PgS2953.htm — Schumer and Rounds original statutory proposal. This is **not** identical to the final version passed by Congress.
- **National Archives record group 615:** https://www.archives.gov/research/topics/uaps — 2024 NDAA sections 1841–1843 established a historical UAP records collection. A statutory category for unknown-origin technology or non-human intelligence does not itself demonstrate those objects exist.
- **Black Vault FOIA researcher:** original archive description and actual government-filed DoD OIG / ODNI records request examples are supplied in the individual profile. A completed FOIA disclosure documents an agency response, not scientific authenticity of a reported event. Do not expose personal requestor contact details from original agency correspondence.

**Claims and people are different:** The 27 decisions say only that the **indexed evidence** establishes a role or research activity, not that a given individual has never made an independent assertion elsewhere. A further claim may be documented without overwriting the role, and only that assertion should be assessed.

**Proposed 0–100 rubric is not a truth probability:** A numeric editorial component review is gated by original statement context, independent support for the *underlying event*, direct inspectable records, testability, alternatives and a named independent second editor. University affiliation, elected office, medals, status as a journalist, government agency letterhead and repetition of the same source never earn points automatically.
