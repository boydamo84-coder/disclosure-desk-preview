## What the preliminary classification means

All 50 listed people remain; 23 have a specific attributed claim or recommendation and now carry both an unsigned first-pass original-source comparison and a draft scoring-suitability decision. The other 27 have explicit documented role/publication-only decisions, not a personal 0/100. Within the 23, six require event-specific review, nine require documentary or source-provenance verification, six require an independently reviewed scientific method or technical inference, and two are **not suitable for an extraordinary-incident evidence percentage** (Mike Gold's policy recommendation and Christopher Mellon's record-release/classification argument). Those two can have a differently labelled source-based policy or records audit later, but they must never be forced into the incident percentage or community incident ballot.

A claim might be well documented as **something a person said** while the alleged underlying technology, movement, biological matter, government programme or classification breach remains unresolved. The categories above prioritise the necessary research. They do not express a probability of truth or a score, and an item with a primary link is not automatically eligible for one.

Scientific basis for this caution: [NASA's UAP scientific study FAQs](https://science.nasa.gov/uap/faqs/) explain that the limited quality and calibration of many observations prevent robust physical identification. Official [NARA's 24 April 2025 release](https://www.archives.gov/press/press-releases/2025/nr25-07) confirms actual lawful record transfers; it is not by itself an audit of alleged wrongful classification. The [AARO March 2024 historical review](https://media.defense.gov/2024/Mar/08/2003409233/-1/-1/0/HRR-VOL-I-REPORT-08-MARCH-2024.PDF) is an institutional perspective with a documented review scope, not a replacement for checking each claimant's original item.

## Non-negotiable editorial signoff checklist

Before any real numerical evidence score is introduced, **each claim** needs a case-specific, independently documented approval record. Each checkbox must be backed by a direct original-source URL and location or a private staff-only custody note where disclosure would breach source confidentiality or rights:

- [ ] Verify the exact original speech, documentary, paper or submitted testimony date, attribution, transcript, completeness, edited segments and the claim's precise scope.
- [ ] Review original primary material, not only excerpts, search indexing, third-party reuploads or an article about the material; record access gaps honestly.
- [ ] Identify the specific empirical fact being assessed separately from a person's sincerity, role, nationality, employer, political activity or fame.
- [ ] Resolve source independence: one newspaper and its syndication is one report; one coauthored sample paper is one experiment; one event reported by two people is not two datasets.
- [ ] Check accessible calibrated sensor measurements, original sample custody, dated official programme records, laboratory controls and uncertainties for the particular claim. If unavailable, do not claim verification.
- [ ] Record credible contrary and ordinary alternatives, their precise original records, limits and any unresolved disagreements without treating one agency's general finding as final case-specific proof.
- [ ] Perform specialist verification when required: optics/radar for Navy clips, analytical chemistry for IM1 or Council Bluffs, statutory legal classification for Mellon, Portuguese independent translation for Varginha.
- [ ] Confirm all external links work for readers, document paywalls and broken videos, and review portrait/media rights, fair quotation, licence attribution and redaction of private source identities.
- [ ] Obtain two named, distinct human editorial approvals with dates and conflict-of-interest review. A first-pass AI-assisted source summary, staff checklist or signed-in user vote is **not** a substitute for independent expert approval.
- [ ] If a quantitative evidence-strength score is genuinely suitable, write all five rubric components (25 original attribution, 30 independent corroboration, 25 direct records, 10 testability, 10 alternative evidence) with individual source IDs, explicit rationale and uncertainty. Do not interpret the total as scientific posterior probability or personal truthfulness.
- [ ] Publish a revision ID, corrections channel, original evidence trail and a clear 'not assessed' state until fully signed off; allow re-review if new evidence changes the conclusion.
- [ ] Separately verify signed-in community voting in an isolated test database: no direct raw vote exposure, owner-scoped RLS, one vote per account/claim, anti-abuse limits, no source review bypass, no average until five votes, and no member identity leakage.
- [ ] Obtain explicit owner deployment authorization after complete no-deploy CI and real-device checks. Confirm the actual Netlify Published SHA and restore/rollback route before release.

## Production and editorial limits

Every `ratingReadiness.publicationApproval` is `withheld`, every second-editor field is `not_assigned`, and every `sourceAudit.reviewerSignoff` is `not_obtained`. The frontend now refuses to render a scored real review while its first-pass readiness remains withheld; future independent approval requires an explicit editorial transition plus all existing original-source, evidence-trail and reviewer checks. Scientific hypotheses and transparent-policy proposals must never be confused with confirmation of alien origin.
