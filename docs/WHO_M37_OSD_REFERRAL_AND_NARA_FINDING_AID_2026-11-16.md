## Finding 1: the Navy's 151 referred pages have not been confirmed released

The [NAVAIR 8 July 2025 letter, FOIA DON-NAVY-2020-007231](https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/navy/2020-007231.pdf), **PDF pp. 1–2 visually checked** (zero-based 0–1), describes a request concerning public placement of **FLIR1, GIMBAL and GOFAST** on the Navy FOIA site.

It distinguishes **34 pages** partly released in four email groups; **498 pages** fully withheld (59 emails and 3 legal memoranda); and **seven other documents, 151 pages**, sent to the Office of the Secretary of Defense (OSD) for its own review and **direct response** to the original requester. The letter is government-authored but the scanned copy inspected here is served by The Black Vault, not an original Navy-hosted download. The FOIA processing totals and the legal exemptions are **not evidence of nonhuman technology**.

The [original requester's 9 July 2025 article](https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/navy-withheld-nearly-500-pages-about-uap-video-release-decision-records-show-foia-pressure-drove-disclosure/) said the OSD review was **pending as of that article's date**, not necessarily pending in November 2026. A further targeted 16 November 2026 public-source search did **not establish** a specific tracking number, final decision, or publicly released 151-page document set associated with the **seven OSD referrals**. This is **not evidence they are still pending**, and it must **not** be phrased as proof that none exists.

### Critical negative result: OSD case 20-F-1095 is **not** established to be that referral

An apparently promising [OSD/JS FOIA release, case 20-F-1095](https://documents2.theblackvault.com/documents/osd/20-F-1095.pdf) has a government-authored final letter dated **18 June 2025**. Text extracted from its original-file PDF index (lines 0–35) says it answers a **16 May 2020** request received 18 May, about *Pentagon public-affairs spokesperson communication*, with **263 responsive pages** in the OSD search. It contains extensive references to FLIR1, GIMBAL and GOFAST; for example the extracted text **lines ~1250–1289** show **a separate 14 October 2020 Navy-to-OSD email** attaching a **DON-NAVY-2020-007231 case file** for redaction consultations.

**Neither the presence of an attachment named 007231, nor an OSD sender, nor a 263-page release proves the 151-page set was separately disposed.** Compare the actual identifiers and purpose:

| Document / search | Case number | Response date | Scope and count | Status of 151-page referral |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NAVAIR public video website-release case | `DON-NAVY-2020-007231` | **8 Jul 2025** | 4 email groups / 34 pages partly released; 498 withheld; **7 docs / 151 pages referred** | Referral formally stated; OSD answer **not verified** |
| OSD/JS spokesperson correspondence case | `20-F-1095` | **18 Jun 2025** | 263 responsive pages to an earlier public-affairs correspondence request | Different request and number; contains 007231 cross-reference, **not proved referral disposition** |
| Navy video-custody inquiry | `DON-NAVY-2020-007330` | 2020 correspondence, not a verified closure letter here | History/custody of a **particular** Navy video and related AFOSI file | Cannot fill either separate release decision or missing 2004 camera master |

The **scope difference and request IDs**, not calendar order by itself, are the strongest reasons to keep the files distinct. The OSD letter precedes the NAVAIR July letter but interagency documents can move before a final agency response; do **not** assume chronology itself makes linkage impossible. A **specific OSD referral/tracking-number notice** and its actual response are needed.

**Access limitation:** The OSD case PDF is about **42.5 MB**, and the web screenshot attempt of its first page failed with the viewer's size limit. A local container download attempt also failed at DNS resolution. Accordingly, the OSD final-letter date and source excerpts were inspected from a **PDF extracted-text index**, not a successfully rendered page image or a downloaded, SHA-256-verified government original. Keep `pdfScreenshotAttempt:FAILED` and no verified original hash; unlike the Navy case's 1–2 page screenshots, **do not describe 20-F-1095 as fully visually audited**.

### A related Navy custody chain is *yet another* FOIA request

Within OSD 20-F-1095's searchable record, a 16 June 2020 chain explicitly concerns **DON-NAVY-2020-007330**, a request for history/custody of video recording cited in an **AFOSI case**. It includes a Navy correspondence view suggesting the videos moved through squadron/fleet and Jay Stratton to PAX **without a releasable paper trail** (approx. PDF-index lines **2630–2643**). Separate lines ~**2255–2258** discuss **VFA-11 and GIMBAL**, and ~**2661–2672** describe the specific FOIA request. This supports a **narrow 2020 administrative account** with uncertain releasable custody documentation; it is **not original evidence of 2004 Nimitz camera master custody**, raw radar data, the identity of a video target, or that *all* Navy offices had no records. The quoted search of certain fleet offices is limited to those offices' responses.

There are therefore **three distinct FOIA tracks**, and repeated appearances of the same email or case attachment do not add independent extraordinary-claim corroboration.

## Finding 2: government archival identifiers for future certified NARA check

The [Reagan Presidential Library's official *Operation Majestic 12* topic guide](https://www.reaganlibrary.gov/public/archives/textual/topics/operamaj.pdf) was examined as a one-page source image. It lists **specific White House Office of Records Management (WHORM) case identifiers** relevant to requests on MJ-12 and a **George A. Keyworth III, White House Science Advisor, RAC Box 16** set of miscellaneous folders:

| Archival category | File identifiers on guide |
|---|---|
| `FE010-01` | **094780, 502752, 517937** |
| `OS` | **052606, 055537, 157693, 495613** |
| `PR014-08` | **446832** |
| White House Science Advisor, Keyworth, 1981–85 | **RAC Box 16; “U & V Miscellaneous (1)” and “(2)”** |

**This is a finding aid, NOT government authentication of a MJ-12 programme.** The guide's visible one-page PDF footer reads **2/12/2024**, whereas its PDF text extraction supplied by the index displays **6/1/2026**. Because these differ, this audit does not confidently assign the guide a single issue date. The inspected **page image** is the basis for the actual file identifiers and physical archive folder labels. The guide does **not explicitly claim** that its listed folders contain the 9 May 1988 Jo Ann Williamson revised report; avoid assuming they do. Ask for the specific report's own series/catalogue reference separately.

The [current National Archives MJ-12 report webpage](https://www.archives.gov/research/military/air-force/ufos) reproduces item 9 with **1956**, while the original government-hosted **1987 report** says **1954** and the privately hosted scanned **9 May 1988 revision** displays underlined **1956** (the six relevant pages were visually inspected in M36). In M37 we still found **no directly NARA-hosted, custody-confirmed version of the revised 1988 report**. Treat its authorship and revision pathway as strongly documented but government certification **not yet obtained**. The new Reagan folder identifiers may help a targeted inquiry; they are **not an additional independent scientific or intelligence confirmation**.
