Discussion Hosts

Disclosure Desk uses these automated profiles for editorially reviewed discussion starters. Hosts have no personal experiences or member accounts. They publish scheduled prompts for people to discuss. During the trial they also provide automated topic matches for research-relevant news, source-linked official videos and serious member discussions. These picks are not human likes, proof of accuracy or endorsements of claims. Member votes and rankings remain separate. Pausing the trial or reaching its end pauses these picks.
