Mike Gold — scientific research proposals

In his November 2024 congressional testimony, Gold proposed examining NASA archival data, improving civilian aviation reporting, involving international partners and considering specialised measurement instruments. He explicitly said he was speaking on his own behalf, not on behalf of NASA or his employer.

  • Original written testimony — House Oversight

Christopher Mellon — UAP records-disclosure argument

    How members will be able to assess evidence (five criteria)

    Disclosure Desk does not publish editorial numerical ratings. The proposed optional community worksheet is about a precisely attributed claim, not a person's trustworthiness or the probability of an extraordinary interpretation. Members will be able to mark a criterion Not enough information instead of guessing; this is different from giving zero points.

    • Original record and full context — up to 25 points
    • Genuinely independent corroboration — up to 30 points
    • Original measurements and documentary provenance — up to 25 points
    • Testability and methodology — up to 10 points
    • Alternative explanations and uncertainty — up to 10 points

    Partial personal worksheets will not be extrapolated to 100. A future public summary would show each criterion's member response distribution, number of respondents and number selecting Not enough information, not a single headline truth percentage. Voting is not active until account-security, privacy, moderation and claim-eligibility checks are complete.

    To suggest a primary source or challenge an assessment, contact the research desk.

          Directory policy

          Profiles are evidence guides, not popularity contests

          Statements are attributed to their speakers or published institutions. Reporting, first-hand testimony, technical analysis and alleged facts are different types of evidence. Inclusion is not an endorsement or accusation.

          We will not label real people as dishonest, part of a covert operation or otherwise malicious based on community opinion. The directory provides original records and open questions rather than a Disclosure Desk evidence score. A future optional member worksheet will apply only to a specific, attributed claim, with private individual answers and safeguards for public aggregates. No ballots are active, and no numerical rating of a person is collected.

          To suggest a person, provide a public source supporting their work. To request a correction, email the research desk with the name, the disputed wording and a primary reference.
