          Directory policy

          Profiles are evidence guides, not popularity contests

          Statements are attributed to their speakers or published institutions. Reporting, first-hand testimony, technical analysis and alleged facts are different types of evidence. Inclusion is not an endorsement or accusation.

          We will not label real people as dishonest, part of a covert operation or otherwise malicious based on a popularity poll. A future optional evidence survey may ask neutral questions about the source material supporting a specific claim, with moderation and anti-manipulation safeguards. No live public polls or vote totals exist in this preview.

          To suggest a person, provide a public source supporting their work. To request a correction, email the research desk with the name, the disputed wording and a primary reference.
