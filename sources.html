Sources & Archives

Trace UFO/UAP reporting and research to original government catalogues, official investigation agencies and independently maintained collections. Search by country, organisation or source type.

Our sourcing standards. A documented report is not proof of an extraordinary explanation. Government records, independent research and first-person accounts are clearly distinguished. We link to original publishers, acknowledge archival references and respect copyright, privacy and corrections. Listing an organisation does not mean it endorses or partners with Disclosure Desk.

Using archived material responsibly

This is a curated directory of collection-level entry points, not a claim that every record is copied into Disclosure Desk or independently verified. Always use the individual document’s identifier, credit the originating archive and check access, reuse, privacy and image rights before republishing. For Brazil’s Objeto Voador Não Identificado fonds, Arquivo Nacional specifically requests the archive name, fonds name and the correct individual reference code on every reference or use. Titles in other languages should retain their original wording alongside any English explanation.
