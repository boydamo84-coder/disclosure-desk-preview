1. Start with the record

USAF personnel reported unusual lights near RAF Woodbridge, Suffolk, in December 1980. An official memorandum by Lt Col Charles Halt, dated January 1981, records reports of those events.

Trace the source

UK National Archives · DEFE 24/1948/1 · Ministry of Defence UFO records

National Archives collection · Read our case dossier

What does this establish? An official report and record trail exist. They do not independently identify the cause of the reported lights.

2. Compare possibilities

Two example hypotheses to investigate, not conclusions. What observations would distinguish them?

A · Ground-based light

A terrestrial light source might account for some of the reported observations.

Test: Compare documented lines of sight, timings and terrain.

Challenge: Reliable evidence of a moving source away from the predicted position would weaken this explanation.

B · Airborne light

A moving airborne source might account for some of the reported observations.

Test: Look for independent timing, trajectory and flight records.

Challenge: A repeatable ground-based match to the documented observations would weaken this explanation.

Neither option is proven here. Other explanations and different causes for different observations remain possible.
