Recover your documents

This advanced option restores a full W.NE backup (.zip). Want to open an ordinary Word file instead? Import Word (.docx).

We check the W.NE backup first, then let you choose which documents to recover. Every recovered document is a new cloud copy; existing work stays unchanged.

1. Choose your W.NE backup

Up to 25 MB ZIP / 60 MB expanded. This will not include private Investigation Boards, device-only files or Google Drive backups.

No backup selected.

2. Choose documents to recover

Select up to 25 at once. Each selection creates a new copy; nothing existing is replaced. You may repeat for the rest.

Only restore ZIPs you trust. This feature requires your signed-in Disclosure Desk account and an internet connection.
